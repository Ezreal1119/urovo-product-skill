## Page 1 - Product Features

### Core Technology

Uses independently developed decoding technology with full intellectual-property rights for rapid reading of 1D and 2D barcodes.

### Large Window Design

The wide scan angle and large reading area are intended to capture a barcode quickly and accurately when it enters the scan window.

### Ultra-Fast Response

The scanner captures barcodes as they approach the window and is designed to avoid duplicate scanning of codes within the field of view.

### Visual Description

The cover uses XYDcode branding and identifies the product as **T500 Fixed Mount Barcode Scanner**. No additional product values are present beyond the feature text.
