# DT610 Series Product Spec Sheet (Std and Pro Editions)

DT610
One Platform. Two Form Factors.
Designed for Different Enterprise Needs.
The Next Generation of Frontline Power
Powered by the Qualcomm Dragonwing™ Q-6690 processor, the DT610 is the world’s first enterprise
mobile computer with fully integrated UHF (RAIN) RFID capabilities. Combining AI-powered processing,
advanced imaging, scanning, RFID, and vision technologies in a lightweight design, DT610 transforms
real-time data into actionable insights to streamline frontline workflows. Available in two models:the
lightweight DT610 Std Edition and the ultra-rugged DT610 Pro Edition—the DT610 delivers flexible
solutions to meet diverse enterprise needs.
