## Page 1 — Series cover

**UROVO** wordmark top right. Title **K388 Pro Series** / **Mobile Labeling Printer**.

Three product photos:

- Large left: **K388 Pro 2’’** — 5.5-inch screen labeling UI, gun grip, 2-inch magazine with yellow latches and orange-ringed door.
- Upper right: **K388 Pro-BT** — no large screen; label exiting the print mouth.
- Lower right: **K388 Pro 4’’** — wider magazine to the right of the grip, 5.5-inch screen.

**Meet the K388 Pro Series** / *Ultra-compact 4-in-1 Device*

K388 Pro is an all-in-one labeling solution designed to eliminate inefficiencies in on-site labeling workflows, such as excessive walking, waiting time, labeling errors, label mix-ups, rework, and delayed data synchronization.

By combining high-speed scanning, printing, label peeling, and application in a single device, it streamlines operations, improves productivity, and ensures real-time data capture with closed-loop accuracy. Ideal for goods receiving, damaged label replacement in retail, returns processing, and inventory management, K388 Pro helps accelerate workflows across retail, warehousing, and logistics operations.
