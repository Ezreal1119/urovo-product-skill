Meet the K388 Pro Series
Ultra-compact 4-in-1 Device
K388 Pro is an all-in-one labeling solution designed to eliminate inefficiencies in on-site labeling
workflows, such as excessive walking, waiting time, labeling errors, label mix-ups, rework, and delayed
data synchronization.
By combining high-speed scanning, printing, label peeling, and application in a single device, it
streamlines operations, improves productivity, and ensures real-time data capture with closed-loop
accuracy. Ideal for goods receiving, damaged label replacement in retail, returns processing, and
inventory management, K388 Pro helps accelerate workflows across retail, warehousing, and logistics
operations.
