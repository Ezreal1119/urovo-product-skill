# SOTI-OEMConfig Guide

> **Canonical original:** `_shared/operations/soti-oemconfig-guide_202608151815/SOTI-OEMConfig-guide.docx`
> **Language:** Chinese
> **Scope:** Step-by-step guide for configuring Urovo OEMConfig via SOTI (MobiControl), with seven embedded screenshots (visual-only; preserved in the original DOCX).

## Configuration Steps

### Step 1 — 设备恢复出厂设置，扫描 SOTI 注册码，连接外网 WiFi

Restore the device to factory settings, scan the SOTI registration code, and connect to an external WiFi network.

### Step 2 — SOTI 注册后等待同步并确认注册成功

After SOTI registration, wait for the device to refresh and synchronize. Check the web console to confirm whether the device has registered successfully.

On the website, follow the steps to find the latest version — at the time this guide was written, the latest version is **1.3.42** — then click "编辑" (Edit).

### Step 3 — 开始配置并下发

Follow the steps to begin configuration. After saving, push (下发) the configuration and select the corresponding device.

### Step 4 — 设备端检查 OEMConfig 是否下载

On the device side, check whether OEMConfig has been downloaded.
