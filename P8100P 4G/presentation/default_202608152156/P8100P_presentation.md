## Intra-source Discrepancy Note

Slide 12 lists the protection rating as **"IP65 dustproof and waterproof"**, while the corresponding spec sheet (`P8100P 4G/specs/default_202601010000/P8100P 4G(EN)-SPEC-20250704.md`) lists **IP67**. The spec sheet value (IP67) is treated as authoritative; this presentation may reflect an earlier draft or a regional variant. **Discrepancy not currently logged in the DT630-style Product Hub Active Local Source Discrepancies table** (only applicable to the product spec pages). For current hard-spec answers, refer to the spec sheet.
