## 3. Disassembly steps (page 5)

Pre-preparation tools for disassembly: a screwdriver, a Phillips screwdriver, tweezers, disassembly sheet.

**Visual — tools (image6.png):** two black screwdrivers (one Phillips, one Torx-like) and metal tweezers on a white background.

**Visual — image7.png:** four blue plastic pry tools; handwritten Chinese **手机拆机片** (phone disassembly picks). Same photo is reused in the i9000S service manual (byte-identical `image2.png` there).

### 1). Remove the battery cover and battery

Loosen the battery cover buckle, battery bayonet, take out the battery cover and battery.

**Visual — image8.png:** tablet rear, red circles on the battery-cover latches / bayonets.

**Visual — image9.png:** battery cover removed; rectangular battery pack still in the well, UROVO on the cover beside it.

## 2). Remove the rear case (page 6)

Use a Torx screwdriver to loosen the screws on the rear case, and remove the rear case through the disassembly piece (**Note: The bottom sticker needs to be torn off before the bottom screw is loosened**).

**Visual — image10.png:** rear case off: battery pack in a red dashed rectangle in the well; detached rear cover (UROVO) and a second cover/plate on the right.

**Visual — image11.png:** many red circles around perimeter screws (~20). Red Chinese overlay **松开底部螺丝前撕开底部贴纸** with an arrow at the bottom-center sticker. Yellow warning triangle sticker near the top of the battery well. NFC coil graphic molded in the rear housing. Battery labels include UROVO barcodes (sample unit; not a catalog SN claim).

## 3). Disassemble the scan engine (page 7)

Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the fixing screws of the scanning head and speaker, loosen the connecting cable, and take out the scanning head, speaker, and flash.

**Visual — image12.png:** rear housing off, internals exposed, scan-engine / speaker area at the top; several small parts laid on the bench above the chassis.

**Visual — image13.png:** close-up of the scan-engine / speaker / flash area with red circles on fixing screws.

## 4). Disassemble the mainrboard (page 8)

Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the motherboard screws, loosen the motherboard connection cable, and take out the motherboard.

**Visual — image14.png / image15.png / image16.png:** green mainboard occupying the center of the chassis; FPC/cables; red circles on board screws. Shield cans and a large EMI cover over the SoC area.

## 5). Disassemble the USB board (page 9)

Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the USB board fixing screw, loosen the connection cable, and take out the USB board.

**Visual — image17.png:** chassis after mainboard removal, remaining interconnects.

**Visual — image18.png:** small green USB I/O daughterboard held above the chassis.
