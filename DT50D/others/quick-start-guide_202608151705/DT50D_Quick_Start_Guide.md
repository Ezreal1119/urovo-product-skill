## Setting Up Your Smart Terminal

### SIM Card / TF Card Installation

Use the silver tab on the top-left corner to pull out the SIM card/TF card holder. Place the SIM and TF cards into the respective slots (as shown), then push the holder in completely.

- TF Slot
- Nano SIM Slot 1
- Nano SIM Slot 2
- Card Tray

Note: SIM card 1 and 2 are Nano SIM cards.

### Battery Installation

1. Install the battery in the direction shown.
2. Place the back cover fully, then push the lock buckle to the right (lock).

NOTE: Avoid overexertion when installing or removing the battery.

## Power Options

- **Power on:** press and hold the power button until the device vibrates.
- **Shutdown:** press and hold until the shutdown prompt appears; click "Shutdown".
- **Forced restart:** press and hold the power button for 10 seconds.

## USB Data Cable Charging

Connect one end of the USB cable to the adapter and the other to the device. A charging cradle is recommended.

## PC Connections

Connect via the USB cable; the PC auto-downloads drivers if needed. Tap to activate the developer menu or USB preference menu.

- SLAVE to PC → "This device" auto-selected.
- HOST to other devices → "Connected device" auto-selected.

On a Windows PC, "My Computer" → "Removable Disk" to transfer data.

## Status Indicators

**Battery:** full, medium, charging, low power.
**Network signal:** good, unstable, none, SIM not installed, SIM error/no service.
**WiFi:** on (no networks), connected, none, switched off.
**NFC:** NFC switched on.

## Precautions

Read the guide; use approved accessories; use within the specified temperature range; do not disassemble; the battery is flammable/explosive; dispose per local regulations.

## Warranty Policy

Free maintenance within one year for material/production quality problems; 12 months device, 3 months accessories; excludes misuse/unauthorized repair; expires if the warranty card is altered.
