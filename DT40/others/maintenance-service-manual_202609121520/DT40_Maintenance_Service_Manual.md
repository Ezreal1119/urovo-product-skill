## 3. Disassembly steps

### 1)、Rear Cover Disassemble (pages 5–6)

First open the battery cover and remove the battery.

Remove the **12 hexagon socket screws** at the bottom of the fuselage.

Gently pry along the gap between the rear case assembly and the front case bracket. It is recommended to pry from one side of the card tray first, then the bottom, then the other side, and finally the top to remove the rear case **(take care to lift up the device information paper first)**.

**Visual:** battery cover / battery removed; **12 red-circled hex screws** on the rear; side-gap pry photo; opened chassis with a red box on the device-information paper in the well.

### 2)、mainboard disassemble (pages 7–10)

First remove the **3 screws** on the PGP board.

Then remove the **4 screws** on the scanning head bracket.

Then pick up the main board, and remove the cable buckle of the scanning head and the rear camera from the main board.

First remove the PGP cable clip, motherboard-keyboard cable clip, front camera cable clip, display cable clip and coaxial cable, and then remove the **2 screws** on the motherboard.

Carefully remove the motherboard.

**Visual:** PGP-board screws circled; scan-head-bracket screws circled; board lifted to show scan-head / rear-camera FPCs; remaining clips (PGP / KB / front camera / display / coaxial) released; isolated mainboard.

### 3)、 USB board and key board disassemble (pages 10–12)

First remove the coaxial cable, and then remove the **4 screws**.

Remove the motherboard-button board cable buckle and the motherboard-USB board cable buckle, and remove the **2 screws** on the USB board.

Then lift the USB board carefully.

Remove the **2 screws** fixing the key board, and then carefully remove the key board.

**Visual:** USB / I/O board along the bottom; coaxial aside; key board (KB) lifted from the keypad stack.

### 4)、scan engine disassemble (page 13)

Remove the screw that fixes the scanning head.

Remove the **2 screws**, remove the scanning head cable, and the scanning head is removed.

**Visual:** scan engine in the bracket; engine + FPC isolated.

### 5)、Rear front camera removal (page 14)

First remove the cable buckle of the rear camera, then carefully lift the rear camera with a tool, and remove the camera.

Unscrew the screws that fix the front camera, and carefully remove the front camera.

**Visual:** rear-camera module lifted; front-camera module and its retaining screw.
