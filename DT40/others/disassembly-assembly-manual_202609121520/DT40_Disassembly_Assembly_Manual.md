## Disassembly steps (pages 2–14)

1. 把电池盖卡扣推到解锁位置，如图2；然后把电池盖往设备底部方向推，如图3  
   Push the battery cover buckle to unlock position, such as picture 2; then push battery cover to the bottom of the device, such as picture 3.

   **Figure 2 — Visual:** keypad handheld rear, battery cover on; red arrow at the cover lock toward the bottom.

   **Figure 3 — Visual:** same rear; red outline around the sliding battery cover.

2. 打开电池盖，取出电池，如图4  
   Open the battery cover and take out the battery, as shown in Figure 4.

   **Figure 4 — Visual:** cover off; black battery pack beside the empty well.

3. 取出电池放水圈，如图5  
   Take out the battery drain ring, as shown in Figure 5.

   **Figure 5 — Visual:** red circle on the battery-well sealing ring / gasket.

4. 取出底壳螺丝12pcs，如图6  
   Take out the bottom case screws 12pcs, as shown in Figure 6.

   **Figure 6 — Visual:** rear chassis, **12 red-circled screws** around the perimeter. Isolated screw shown beside the unit.

5. 使用拆机片打开底壳，如图7  
   Use the dismantling sheet to open the bottom case, as shown in Figure 7.

   **Figure 7 — Visual:** rear housing separated from the front assembly; white arrow from the detached rear cover toward the open chassis.

6. 取底壳拆除电池仓贴纸,耳机板，BOX，马达，MIC 套，如图8  
   Remove the bottom case and remove the battery compartment sticker, headphone board, BOX, motor, MIC cover, as shown in Figure 8.

   **Figure 8 — Visual:** open chassis; red boxes on the headphone board, speaker BOX, motor, and MIC boot.

7. 拆4pcs 螺丝取出主天线支架，如图9  
   Remove the 4pcs screws and take out the main antenna bracket, as shown in Figure 9.

   **Figure 9 — Visual:** four screws at the antenna-bracket area; bracket lifted.

8. 拆主FPC BTB 端子,然后拆2pcs 螺丝取出SUB 板，如图10  
   Remove the main FPC BTB terminal, then remove the 2pcs screws to take out the SUB board, as shown in Figure 10.

   **Figure 10 — Visual:** two-step photo — BTB unlatched; SUB board removed from the bottom of the chassis.

9. 拆RF 线，拆KB 板上2PCS 螺丝，取出KB 板，如图11  
   Remove the RF cable, remove the 2PCS screws on the KB board, and take out the KB board, as shown in Figure 11.

   **Figure 11 — Visual:** keyboard / KB board lifted; RF cable disconnected.

10. 拆扫描头支架上4pcs 螺丝,取出扫描头支架，如图12  
    Remove the 4pcs screws on the scan head bracket and take out the scan head bracket, as shown in Figure 12.

    **Figure 12 — Visual:** scan-head bracket screws circled; bracket lifted.

11. 取扫描头支架,拆LED 灯板2pcs 螺丝，取出LED 灯板,拆PGP 板上3pcs 螺丝，取出PGP 板,然后再拆扫描头2pcs 螺丝，取出Scaner,最后取出后CAM，如图13  
    Take the scanning head bracket, remove the 2pcs screws of the LED light board, take out the LED light board, remove the 3pcs screws on the PGP board, take out the PGP board, then remove the 2pcs screws of the scanning head, take out the Scaner, and finally take out the rear CAM, as shown in Figure 13.

    **Figure 13 — Visual:** exploded scan-head area with numbered callouts **1** and **2** on the LED / PGP / engine stack.

12. 拆主板2pcs 螺丝,拆RF 同轴线,拆主板端FPC 连接器端子，如图14  
    Remove the 2pcs screws from the motherboard, remove the RF coaxial cable, and remove the FPC connector terminals on the motherboard, as shown in Figure 14.

    **Figure 14 — Visual:** mainboard screws and FPC/coaxial seats highlighted.

13. Remove the front camera pressing steel screw and steel sheet, the front camera, remove the 2pcs light guide, and the receiver, remove the left and right scan keys FPC, remove the main waterproof ring, as shown in Figure 15.

    **Figure 15 — Visual:** front-shell internals after the mainboard is out — front camera steel sheet, light guides, receiver, scan-key FPCs, main waterproof ring.

14. Take out the bottom case, remove the scan key button 2pcs, 2pcs button conductive base, remove the volume key, PTT key, power button, and take out the key conductive base, as shown in Figure 16.

    **Figure 16 — Visual:** rear/bottom housing with side keys and conductive pads removed.

## 三、装机 / Assembly device (pages 15–27)

1. 装好听筒,导光柱,扫描按键FPC 2pcs,前CAM,前摄像头压紧钢片,主防水圈,如图17  
   Install the earpiece, light guide column, scan button FPC 2pcs, front CAM, front camera pressing steel sheet, and main waterproof ring, as shown in Figure 17.

   **Figure 17 — Visual:** front shell with those parts seated; red boxes on the installed locations.

2. 装主板,锁上2pcs 主板螺丝,扣好前摄像头端子，依次分别扣好后CAM,PGP 板子,主FPC 端子,RF 同轴线，屏FPC 端子,如图18  
   Install the main board, lock the 2pcs main board screws, fasten the front camera terminals, and fasten the rear CAM, PGP board, main FPC terminal, RF coaxial cable, and screen FPC terminal in turn, as shown in Figure 18.

   **Figure 18 — Visual:** mainboard seated; connector order called out.

3. 加工扫描头支架，将扫描头锁上2pcs 螺丝固定在支架上，LED 灯板锁上2pcs 螺丝固定在支架上，如图19  
   Process the scan head bracket, lock the scan head with 2pcs screws and fix it on the bracket, and lock the LED light board with 2pcs screws and fix it on the bracket, as shown in Figure 19.

   **Figure 19 — Visual:** scan-head + LED board assembled onto the bracket; red box on the screw group.

4. 装扫描头支架,并锁上4pcs 螺丝，然后装好后CAM，锁PGP 板子3pcs 螺丝，如图20  
   Install the scanning head bracket, and lock the 4pcs screws, then install the rear CAM, and lock the 3pcs screws of the PGP board, as shown in Figure 20.

   **Figure 20 — Visual:** bracket and PGP board installed in the chassis.

5. 装KB 板,锁上2pcs 螺丝,然后扣RF 线，如图21  
   Install the KB board, lock the 2pcs screws, and then buckle the RF cable, as shown in Figure 21.

   **Figure 21 — Visual:** KB board seated; two screws circled.

6. 装SUB 板,锁上2PCS 螺丝,扣上主FPC 两个BTB 端子,最后装上主MIC 套;如图22  
   Install the SUB board, lock the 2PCS screws, fasten the two BTB terminals of the main FPC, and finally install the main MIC sleeve; as shown in Figure 22.

   **Figure 22 — Visual:** SUB board at the bottom; BTB seated; MIC boot installed.

7. 装主天线支架,然后锁上4pcs 支架螺丝,如图23  
   Install the main antenna bracket, and then lock the 4pcs bracket screws, as shown in Figure 23.

   **Figure 23 — Visual:** antenna bracket installed; four screws circled.

8. 底壳加工,装上开机键,PTT 键，音量键，扫描键导电基和按键；如图24  
   Bottom shell processing, install the power button, PTT button, volume button, scan button conductive base and buttons; as shown in Figure 24.

   **Figure 24 — Visual:** rear housing with side keys and conductive bases installed; screws circled along the top edge.

9. Install the headphone board, BOX speaker, and lock the 4pcs screws to fix it, the motor, and the auxiliary MIC sleeve, as shown in Figure 25.

   **Figure 25 — Visual:** headphone board / speaker BOX / motor / MIC boot in the rear housing.

9. 将底壳装配到面壳上,并锁上12pcs 螺丝固定，贴电池仓贴纸，装电池防水圈，如图26  
   Assemble the bottom case to the top case, and lock the 12pcs screws to fix it, paste the battery compartment sticker, and install the battery waterproof ring, as shown in Figure 26.

   **Figure 26 — Visual:** fully closed rear (battery well open); **12 red-circled screws**; regulatory sticker in the well.

10. 装电池和电池盖，并电池盖锁扣在锁紧状态，如图27  
    Install the battery and battery cover, and lock the battery cover lock, as shown in Figure 27.

    **Figure 27 — Visual:** closed battery cover; lock in the locked position (red box on the lock).
