## Accessories Overview

- **Adaptor**, **USB Cable**, **Battery**
- **Single Charging Cradle**, **4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle**, **4-Slot Cradle**
- **Hand Strap**, **Screen Protector**, **Gun Grip**, **Holster**, **Boot Case**

## Accessories List

### Adaptor / USB Cable / Battery

- **Battery:** removable 3.85 V, 4500 mAh
  - BTY-DT40-45-06, BTY-DT40-45-07, BTY-DT40-45-08, BTY-DT40-45-09
- **USB Cable:** supports charging and data transferring; port: Type-C
- **Adaptor:** input 100–240 V 50/60 Hz 0.6 A MAX; output 5 V/2 A (appearance varies by region)
- **Hand Strap:** convenient for mobile operation and carrying; HS-DT40-02

### Single Charging Cradle

- Supports charging one device and one battery
- LED charging status indicator
- Boot case is not supported
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/2 A
- Chinese standard adapter: CRD-DT40-03; US standard: CRD-DT40-04; European standard: CRD-DT40-05; British standard: CRD-DT40-06

### 4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle

- Supports charging 4 batteries
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Chinese standard adapter: CRD-DT40-01; US standard: CRD-DT40-07; European standard: CRD-DT40-08

### 4-Slot Cradle

- Supports charging 4 devices
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Chinese standard adapter: CRD-DT40-02; US standard: CRD-DT40-09

### Gun Grip (DT40C is not applicable)

- Efficient scanning with trigger button
- Comfortable grip
- UROVO Chinese packaging: TRG-DT40-01; English packaging: TRG-DT40-02

### Screen Protector / Holster / Boot Case

- **Screen Protector:** improves protection performance
- **Holster:** easy to carry around; standard hardware shoulder strap and waist clip; supports one device and one spare battery
- **Boot Case:** enhances device protection; material: TPU
  - Direct scanning head: BC-DT40-01
  - Oblique scanning head: BC-DT40-03
