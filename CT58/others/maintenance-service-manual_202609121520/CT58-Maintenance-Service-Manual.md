## 3. Disassembly steps

### 1）. Removal of the back cover (page 4)

First remove the **10 hexagon socket screws** at the bottom of the fuselage and remove the card tray.

Gently pry along the gap between the rear shell assembly and the front shell bracket to remove the rear shell.

**Visual:** rear of a CT58-class unit, **10 red-circled hex screws** around the perimeter (including the scan-hood pair). Card tray removed. Rear shell then separated from the front-shell / display assembly.

### 2）. Motherboard disassembly (pages 5–6)

First remove all the cables from the motherboard, then remove the camera bracket.

Remove the screws on the motherboard and then remove the motherboard.

**Visual:** opened chassis with cables still on the board; camera bracket lifted; isolated mainboard with a large copper / shield can and a UROVO barcode sticker.

### 3）. Scan head disassembly (pages 6–7)

Remove the screws holding the scan head.

Finally, separate the scan head and receiver cable, remove the **2 screws**, tear off the conductive cloth, and the scan head will be removed.

**Visual:** scan-engine area on the chassis; engine + earpiece cable separated; conductive cloth peeled.

### 4）. Rear and front camera disassembly (page 7)

First remove the cable buckle of the front and rear cameras, then use a tool to carefully lift the front and rear cameras and remove the cameras.

**Visual:** camera FPC latches open; cameras lifted from the bracket.

### 5）. USB board removal (page 8)

First unplug the coaxial cable from the USB board, put the coaxial cable aside, and remove the USB cover and the **4 screws** on the USB board.

Remove the motherboard-USB board cable buckle, carefully lift the USB board and remove it.

**Visual:** long USB / I/O board along the bottom of the chassis; coaxial cable unplugged; board lifted.

### 6）. Things to note when replacing the display screen (page 9)

When replacing a new display screen, the **light blocking column** and **light sensor** under the motherboard must not be left behind.

**Visual:** display assembly in the front shell; callout at the light-sensor / light-pipe under the board area.
