### Safety Precautions

- Only use the manufacturer-specified charger. Do not disassemble, impact, crush, or throw the battery into fire. Discontinue use if the battery becomes severely swollen. Do not expose to high temperatures, and do not use after water immersion.
- Do not store the battery in places exceeding 70 °C (158 °F), such as inside vehicles parked in hot weather under direct sunlight, near radiators, or other heat sources.
- Do not use the battery if it is noticeably deformed, swollen, or discolored; replace it immediately.
- Do not use the battery if it is too hot to handle; replace and discard it.
- Do not disassemble, open, crush, bend, deform, puncture, or shred the battery.
- Do not drop the battery or apply mechanical shock or pressure.
- Do not short-circuit the battery or allow metal or conductive objects to touch its terminals.
- Do not throw the battery into fire or expose it to high-temperature environments like microwaves or dryers.
- Do not modify or refurbish the battery, insert foreign objects, submerge it in liquid, or expose it to fire/explosion hazards.
- Keep the charging area free of debris, flammable materials, or chemicals; take extra care in non-commercial environments.
- Store batteries safely out of the reach of children.
- Follow local regulations for disposal; cover battery terminals with electrical insulating tape before disposal.
- If a battery is accidentally swallowed, seek medical attention immediately.
- If the battery leaks, avoid contact with the liquid; if contact occurs, rinse with plenty of water and seek medical advice.

### Use and Storage

- Configure screen timeout to turn off the screen early and enable adaptive brightness to reduce battery usage.
- Disable unnecessary background applications; use Battery Saver and Battery Management to conserve power; enable Airplane Mode when network connectivity is unnecessary.
- Lithium-ion battery capacity declines over time due to repeated charge/discharge cycles and environmental factors.
- Charge in an environment between -20 °C (-4 °F) and 60 °C (140 °F); avoid extreme temperatures.
- Use the manufacturer-specified charger; incompatible batteries or chargers may cause fire, explosion, or leakage.
- Replace batteries regularly, typically every two years, or if battery health falls below 70%.
- When the low-power icon appears, charge immediately or replace with a spare; avoid completely depleting the battery.
- Avoid overcharging; disconnect power once fully charged.
- For storage of several days, insert a fully charged battery and activate Airplane Mode or power off.
- For battery storage, charge to approximately 50% at around 25 °C (77 °F).
- For longer storage, remove the battery after charging and store the battery and device separately in a cool, dry location; periodically check the charge level.

### Charge the Battery Before Use

The DT630 has a removable battery. Charge the battery for at least 3 hours before use with the supplied adapter and cable or battery dock charger. Using the device while charging increases the time for a full charge. The battery is not installed in a freshly unpacked unit.

1. Connect the charging accessory to the appropriate power source.
2. Insert the DT630 into a cradle or attach a USB cable. The Charging/Notification LED is red and turns green when fully charged.

Charging methods: charging with cradle, or charging with USB cable.

The indicator light is at the top right of the front of the device:

| State | LED | Indication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | — | DT630 is not charging; not inserted correctly in the cradle or connected to a power source; the charger/cradle is not powered |
| Long red light | Red | DT630 is charging |

## Chapter 2 — Using the Device

### Installing SD/SIM Card

1. Remove the battery cover and battery.
2. Insert the TF card / SIM2 card into the card slot (as shown). SIM1 card and TF card are separate slots.
3. Install the battery.

Note: the SIM card is a Nano-SIM card. Keep the card and hands dry when installing, and keep the card surface clean to avoid poor contact.

### Turn On/Off DT630

**Turn on:** long press the power key; when DT630 enters startup mode it vibrates; release the power key and wait until booting finishes. If it does not turn on: if the battery is running low, charge first; or press and hold the power key for over 10 seconds to force reboot.

**Turn off / restart:** press and hold the power button until the shutdown prompt appears, then click "Power off". Touch to restart.

### Un-locking the Screen

Use the lock screen to protect access to data; some email accounts require locking. A pattern, PIN, or password is required to unlock. The screen lock automatically activates every time the display turns off.

**Setting method:** Open "Settings – Device unlock – Password / Face and Fingerprint".

- For "Password": None, Swipe, Pattern, PIN, Password.
- For "Face and Fingerprint": Pattern·Fingerprint·Face, PIN·Fingerprint·Face, Password·Fingerprint·Face.
- Follow the on-screen prompts to complete setup.

- **Pattern screen:** unlock with pattern.
- **Password screen:** unlock with password.
- **PIN screen:** entering the PIN/password/pattern incorrectly five times requires a 30-second wait; if forgotten, contact the system administrator.
- **Fingerprint screen:** unlock with the fingerprint sensor on the back side, capturing multiple areas of your finger.

## Chapter 4 — Use the Scan Engine

### About the Image Engine

The DT630 supports 360° barcode scanning and provides an aiming beam. Before scanning, go to Settings > Enterprise featured setting > Scanner Settings or ScanDemo > Setting and enable only the barcode symbologies needed to improve scanning speed.

1. Point the scanner window toward the barcode; slightly angle to avoid reflections.
2. Press and hold either (or both) scan buttons to activate the aiming beam.
3. When the device beeps, the barcode was read successfully and the information is entered into the active application.

### Scanner Settings

Note: the OCR settings menu option is only displayed when a license is installed.

- **Suspension button:** opens the virtual key scanning tool USCAN for dragging and touching to simulate key scanning.
- Supports changing the form of triggered scanning (e.g., continuous scanning).
- Setup multi-barcode specific parameters.
- **Output mode:** Intent output, Keystroke output, Keystroke and Intent output.
- Additional functions: Formatting, Prefix, Suffix, Substitute, Delete Non-printing Control Character, App Identifier, or Code Id Type.
- Characters delay, key event delay.

### ScanWedge Profiles

ScanWedge sends scanned barcode data to an app as keypad input. Click "Default" to modify scan settings for the default application. To create new settings and associate apps: (a) click the top-right option and select "New profile"; (b) name the new scan configuration; (c) touch "Associated Apps"; (d) touch "Associate more app"; (e) select the app to associate.

### Floating Action Button

Suspension button: opens the virtual key scanning tool USCAN.

### Triggering Mode

- **Decode or Time-out:** manual trigger; the light stays on until decoding completes or scanning times out.
- **Hands-free continuous:** manual trigger to start continuous scanning for multiple barcodes; press the scan key again to stop.
- **Trigger release or Time-out:** release the key and the light goes out; it also goes out after decoding.
- **Continuous trigger mode:** emits a targeting beam only while the scan button is pressed; release to end scanning.
- **Trigger aiming & loose for scanning:** press the scan button to start; scanning ends once the barcode is read successfully.

### Keystroke Output

Check this mode to output results to the app focus edit box by keyboard.

- **Keyboard type:** Auto text mode, Soft keyboard, Always Physical keyboard. Auto text mode: if a focus input box arises, it shows all data directly, otherwise outputs character by character. Soft keyboard: displays all data via input method editor simulation. Always Physical keyboard: outputs via physical key event simulation at any interface.
- **Sounds:** None, Instant (default), or Squeak.
- **Vibrate:** vibrate after scanning.
- **Action key character:** None, Carriage return, Line feed, Tab (adds operational key characters behind results; None: no operation; Carriage return: add an enter; Line feed: add an OK operation; Tab: add a Tab key).

### Reader Params

- **Coding format:** Chinese code type is UTF-8 by default; must match the 2D code type to avoid garbling.
- **Beam timer:** 500–10000 ms (5000 ms default).
- **Linear code type security level:** higher level is more accurate but slower.
- **Exposure Settings–Lights Configuration:** Illum/Aimer — Off, Aimer Only, Illum Only, Alternating, Concurrent. Exposure Mode: auto/on; Fixed Exposure: 1–900 (higher is brighter).

### Basic Data Formatting

Additional functions: Formatting, Prefix, Suffix, Substitute, Delete Non-printing Control Character, App Identifier, or Code Id Type.

1. Tap "Label Formatting" and select the type.
2. Suffix/Prefix: select mode and modify content (e.g., [CR] = enter); choose a default type or define any character.
3. Replace: input the former character in ASCII code (e.g., "1" → "31").
4. Delete Non-printing Control Character.
5. App Identifier: add to some bar codes (e.g., Code 128).
6. App Delimited Identifier: "()" default, substitutable.
7. Code Id Type: None, Aim, or Symbol.

## Chapter 5 — Use the RFID

The DT630 next-generation RFID module leverages cutting-edge radio frequency technology for seamless tag communication:

- RFID tag maximum read range of **1.2 m (4 ft)**.
- RFID read speed of **50 tags per second**.
- RFID is **disabled by default** and enabled/disabled via RFID SDK APIs.
- RFID and WWAN can co-exist.

### Optimal Read Range

- Adjust positioning to establish the ideal read distance.
- Tag size matters — larger tags require slightly greater separation.
- High-density tags perform better with closer proximity.

### Antenna Orientation

- Avoid signal interference by positioning the reader antenna at an angle to prevent signal reflection.
- A 15–30° tilt often improves detection; extreme angles may weaken signal strength.
- Metal surfaces and liquids may require adjusted positioning.

### Performance Tips

- Movement control: slow, steady motions improve read rates during inventory scans.
- Tag placement: affix tags to flat, non-metallic surfaces when possible.
- Interference mitigation: distance the reader from other RF sources (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth) when high-priority scanning is required.

IMPORTANT: avoid placing hands and fingers on the RFID antenna for optimal performance.

## Chapter 8 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting

### Maintenance Equipment

- Do not scratch the screen; the screen is glass — do not drop or subject to strong impact.
- Protect from temperature extremes; do not leave on a car dashboard on a hot day.
- Do not store or use anywhere dusty, damp, or wet.
- Clean with a soft lens cloth; if soiled, use a soft cloth moistened with diluted window-cleaning solution.

**Long-term storage:** battery at least 50% charge, stored at -40–70 °C.

### Cleaning Equipment

Materials: alcohol wipes, lens tissue, cotton-tipped applicators, isopropyl alcohol, can of compressed air with a tube.

- **Housing:** wipe with alcohol wipes including buttons.
- **Display:** wipe with alcohol wipes, avoid liquid pooling, dry with a soft non-abrasive cloth.
- **Camera and exit window:** wipe with lens tissue.
- **Connector cleaning:** dip the cotton-tipped applicator in isopropyl alcohol; rub across the connector; repeat at least three times; use alcohol to remove grease/dirt; use a dry applicator; spray compressed air about ½ inch away; inspect and repeat.
- **Cradle connectors:** remove DC power cable; dip applicator in alcohol; rub along the pins; rub all sides; spray compressed air; remove lint; use lint-free cloth and alcohol for other areas; allow 10–30 minutes to air dry before powering.
