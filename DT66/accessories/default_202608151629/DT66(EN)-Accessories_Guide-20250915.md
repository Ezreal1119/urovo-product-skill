# DT66 Accessories Guide

> **Canonical original:** `DT66/accessories/default_202608151629/DT66(EN)-Accessories_Guide-20250915.pdf`
> **Document date:** 2025-09-15 (from the source filename)
> **Language:** English
> **Scope:** Accessories for the DT66 Enterprise Mobile Computer.

## Accessories Overview

- **Adaptor**, **USB Cable**, **Battery**
- **Single Charging Cradle**, **4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle**, **5-Slot Charging Cradle (5 devices)**, **5-Slot Charging Cradle (4 devices, 4 batteries)**, **Trigger Handle**
- **Hand Strap**, **Screen Protector**, **Rugged Boot**, **Dust Plugs**, **Vehicle Mount Kit**

## Accessories List

### Adaptor / USB Cable / Battery

- **Adaptor:** input 100–240 V ~ 50/60 Hz 0.6 A MAX; output 5 V/2 A; optional QC3.0 adaptor output (5 V/3 A, 9 V/2 A, 12 V/1.5 A); appearance varies by region
- **USB Cable:** supports charging and data transferring; port: USB Type-C
- **Battery:** removable 3.85 V, 5000 mAh; BTY-DT66-02

### Single Charging Cradle

- Supports host charging with or without rugged boot
- Supports charging one device and one battery
- Port: DC jack ×1, Type-C ×1, RJ45 ×1 (for network connection)
- LED charging status indicator
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/2 A
- Chinese standard: CRD-DT66-01; European standard: CRD-DT66-02; US standard: CRD-DT66-03; British standard: CRD-DT66-12

### 4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle

- Supports charging 4 batteries
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Chinese standard: CRD-DT66-04; European standard: CRD-DT66-05; US standard: CRD-DT66-06

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle (5 devices)

- Supports charging 5 devices with or without rugged boot
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Chinese standard: CRD-DT66-07; European standard: CRD-DT66-08; US standard: CRD-DT66-09

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle (4 devices, 4 batteries)

- Supports charging 4 devices (with or without rugged boot) and 4 batteries
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/10 A
- Chinese standard: CRD-DT66-10; European standard: CRD-DT66-11; US standard: CRD-DT66-13

### Trigger Handle

- Efficient scanning with trigger button
- Comfortable grip
- Supports single charging cradle and 5-slot charging cradle
- Chinese package: TRG-DT66-01; English package: TRG-DT66-02

### Hand Strap / Screen Protector

- **Hand Strap:** convenient for mobile operation and carrying; material: microfiber leather + alloy; HS-DT66-01
- **Screen Protector:** improves protection performance; material: tempered glass; SPF-DT66-02

### Rugged Boot

- Two-color injection molding / TPU material
- Supports hand strap
- BC-DT66-01

### Dust Plugs

- Tethered/lanyard-style dust plug for protecting the device opening when it is not in use; the source overview shows its physical form beside the rugged boot.

### Vehicle Mount Kit

- Holder with USB cable and cigarette lighter adaptor included
- Vehicle RAM mount or plate excluded; assembled via ball joint (ball joint diameter 25 mm)
- CAK-DT66-01
