## 1. Disassembly steps (page 2)

Prepare the disassembly tools in advance: a six-point plum screwdriver, a cross screwdriver, tweezers, and a disassembly piece.

**Visual — tools:** same two screwdrivers + tweezers; four blue **手机拆机片**.

### 1. Remove the battery cover and battery

Release the battery cover buckle and remove the battery cover and battery.

**Visual:** rear of a handheld POS with printer at the top, hand-strap; battery cover off; battery beside the unit. Red circles on cover/battery-well screws (8 around the printer/strap area in a later photo).

## 2. Remove the back cover (page 3)

Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the 8 screws on the back cover and remove the back cover using the disassembly tool. (**Note: There are 4 screws inside the printer compartment, 2 screws at the battery position, and 2 screws at the bottom**)

**Visual:** printer clamshell open (receipt printer); back cover removed showing green mainboard; red boxes/circles on remaining screws.

### 3. Disassemble the motherboard

Use a cross screwdriver to loosen the 7 screws on the motherboard, loosen the cable connecting the back of the motherboard to the LCD display, and remove the motherboard.

**Visual:** mainboard in chassis; board lifted to show LCD FPC; board removed.

### 4. Remove the magnetic head (pages 3–4)

Use a cross screwdriver to loosen the two screws that secure the magnetic head, loosen the cable connecting the magnetic head to the motherboard, and remove the magnetic head.

**Visual:** MSR head module at the top of the printer/MSR area; two screws circled.

### 5. Disassemble the printer (page 4)

Use a cross screwdriver to loosen the two screws that secure the printer, loosen the cable connecting the printer to the motherboard, and the cable connecting the scanning head to the motherboard, and take out the printer.

**Visual:** printer mechanism with FPC; printer removed as a module.

### 6. Disassemble the scanning head (page 4)

Use a cross screwdriver to loosen the two screws that secure the scanning head and remove the scanning head.

**Visual:** scan engine with FPC ribbon.

## 7. Remove the rear camera, flash, and speaker (page 5)

Use a cross screwdriver to loosen the two screws on the bracket, loosen the rear camera and motherboard cables, speaker cables, flash cables, and motor cables. (**Note: The speaker cable, flash cable, and motor cable need to be removed by electric iron.**)

**Visual:** camera/flash/speaker/motor cluster; soldered wires noted; red circles on bracket screws.

### 8. Remove the IC card board

Loosen the IC card board fixing bracket and remove the IC card board.

**Visual:** green IC-card daughterboard and metal/plastic bracket; empty rear housing showing speaker grille and screw bosses (marking **S02776** visible in the plastic — sample tooling mark, not a catalog model).
