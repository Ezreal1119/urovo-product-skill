## 2. Disassembly steps

### 1）、Rear Cover Removal (pages 5–6)

First remove the **10 hexagon socket screws** at the bottom of the fuselage, and remove the card tray.

Gently pry along the gap between the rear case assembly and the front case bracket to remove the rear case.

**Visual:** rear of a DT50-class phablet; perimeter hex screws; card tray out; rear shell separated from the front-shell / display assembly.

### 2）、 Motherboard removal (pages 6–7)

First remove all the cables on the motherboard from the motherboard, and then remove the camera bracket.

Remove the screws on the motherboard and remove the motherboard.

**Visual:** opened chassis with cables on the board; camera bracket lifted; isolated mainboard (shield can + UROVO barcode sticker).

### 3）、Scan engine removal (pages 8–9)

Remove the screws securing the scan head.

Finally, separate the scanning head from the earpiece cable, remove the **2 screws**, and the scanning head is removed.

**Visual:** scan engine in the bracket; engine separated from the earpiece cable.

### 4）、Rear camera removal (pages 9–10)

First remove the cable buckle of the rear cameras, then carefully lift the rear cameras with a tool, and remove the camera.

**Visual:** rear-camera FPC latch open; camera lifted.

### 5）、USB board removal (page 10)

First unplug the coaxial cable on the USB board, put the coaxial cable aside, and remove the **2 screws** on the USB board.

Remove the motherboard-USB board cable buckle, carefully lift up the USB board and remove it.

**Visual:** USB / I/O board at the bottom; coaxial aside; board lifted. Red circle on a remaining fastener in one photo.

### 6）、Precautions when replacing the display (page 11)

When replacing a new display screen, the **light blocking column** and **light sensor** under the main board cannot fall down.

**Visual:** display assembly in the front shell; callout at the light-sensor / light-pipe under the board.
