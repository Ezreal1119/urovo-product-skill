## Setting Up Your Smart Terminal

### SIM Card / TF Card Installation

Use the silver tab on the top-left corner to pull out the SIM card/TF card holder. Place the SIM card and TF card into the respective slots (as shown), then push the holder in completely.

- TF Slot
- Nano SIM Slot 1
- Nano SIM Slot 2
- Card Tray

Note: SIM card 1 and 2 are Nano SIM cards.

### Battery Installation

1. Install the battery in the direction shown.
2. Place the back cover fully, then push the lock buckle to the right (lock).

NOTE: Avoid overexertion when installing or removing the battery.

## Power Options

- **Power on:** press and hold the power button until the device vibrates.
- **Shutdown:** press and hold the power button until the shutdown prompt appears; click "Shutdown".
- **Forced restart:** press and hold the power button for 10 seconds (do not press the shutdown prompt); the device will restart.

## USB Data Cable Charging

Connect one end of the USB cable to the adapter and the other end to the device. A charging cradle is recommended for convenient charging.

## PC Connections

Connect the device to the PC using the provided USB cable. If an additional driver is required, the PC will automatically download it from the internet. Once the USB port is connected, drag the notification bar from the top of the screen to display the USB setting interface.

1. Tap to activate the developer menu.
2. Tap to activate the USB preference menu.

USB preference options: "This device" (access device storage), "Connected device" (share device data with the computer), image transfer, and charging only.

Notes:

1. When the device acts as a SLAVE connecting to a PC, the "This device" option is automatically selected.
2. When the device acts as a HOST connecting to other devices, the "Connected device" option is automatically selected.

On a Windows PC, go to "My Computer" and click "Removable Disk" to view and transfer device data.

## Status Indicators

**Battery indicator:** full power, medium power, charging, low power.

**Network signal:** good signal, unstable signal, none, SIM card not installed, SIM card error/no service.

**WiFi:** WiFi on (no available wireless networks for connection), WiFi connected, none, WiFi switched off.

**NFC:** NFC switched on.
