## Chapter 1 — Getting Started

### 1.1 Unpacking

1. Carefully remove all protective materials around DT50 and keep the device case for future storage and transport.
2. Check the package for: DT50 intelligent terminal ×1, adapter ×1, USB cable ×1, quick start guide ×1.
3. Inspect the device for damage; if anything is damaged or missing, contact customer service or the local distributor.
4. Remove the screen shipping protective film before use.

### 1.2 Appearance

- Front side (Figure 1-1), top view (Figure 1-2), bottom view (Figure 1-3), left & right-side views (Figure 1-4), and back view (Figure 1-5).

### 1.3 Installation Guide

**Instruction of battery:** note the direction of installation. Load the front end of the battery into the battery slot and push the bottom into the device; do not push with force. The installed battery should be flat and secure. After installing the battery cover, turn the battery cover fastener right to the lock position.

**Instruction of battery replacement:**

1. Long press the power key; a dialog box pops up.
2. Select power-off.
3. Upon power-off, turn the battery cover fastener left and remove the cover.
4. Lift the battery from its tail end to remove it and replace with a new one.

**Instruction on SIM/TF card replacement:**

1. Long press the power key; a dialog box pops up.
2. Select power-off.
3. Upon power-off, turn the battery cover fastener left and remove the cover.
4. Lift the battery from its tail end to remove it.
5. Manually pull out the SIM/TF card holder in the left of the shell to remove the card.
6. Push a new SIM/TF card into the holder; a slight click indicates installation is complete.

### 1.4 Caution on Battery Charging

1. Do not use or charge the device in any environment with too high or low temperature.
2. Charge the battery only by the product's standard-configuration charger or desktop charger.
3. If the device (including the battery) is not used for the moment, store the battery and device separately, packed in PE bags or other insulated bags in the original package. Do not store unpacked batteries in a stack.
4. Do not idle the device for over 3 months; charge and discharge an unused battery once per 3 months, keeping it 60%–70% charged (i.e., 3.8 V–3.9 V).
5. Store the lithium battery somewhere cool, dry, and ventilated (0–28 °C advised).

### 1.5 Charging Method

**Charge by USB:** for the Type-C USB cable, link one end to a computer and the other to the device. While charging, the charge indicator is red and turns green when fully charged.

**Charge by desktop charger:** the desktop charger can charge the device (including the battery) and a battery at the same time.

1. Connect the DC interface of the base to the power adapter.
2. Load the front end of the battery into the battery slot and push the bottom into the slot.
3. Put the back of the device body into the body slot of the base. If the device indicator is red, charging is underway.

### 1.6 Battery Optimization

- Clear the background of all apps to enter power-saving mode.
- In "Settings–Automatic On/Off", set DT50 to automatically power on/off.
- Reduce screen brightness.
- Turn off unused wireless connections (touch airplane mode; the airplane icon appears in the status bar).
- Turn off automatic updates of e-mail, calendar, contacts, and other apps.

### 1.7 Usage of Touchscreen

- **Light touch** — select options, enter letters/signs, press screen buttons.
- **Light touch press and hold** — create a home-screen shortcut, open the home-screen menu, blank out the home screen until the menu is seen.
- **Drag** — light touch, press and hold, and move the option to a new position.
- **Slide** — browse app icons; set the unlocking pattern on the lock screen.
- **Long press** — call the MENU function on the home screen; drag any app or tool shortcut.

## Chapter 2 — Device Settings

### 2.1 Access Device Settings

- **Method 1:** pull down the status bar to open the quick access panel, then tap the settings icon.
- **Method 2:** on the home screen, pull up the screen to enter the app center interface, then tap the settings icon.

### 2.2 WLAN

After the wireless function is enabled, DT50 automatically displays all available wireless networks in the list. Click the network's SSID and tap CONNECT. For some networks, a security key or password is needed; contact the network admin.

1. Open "Settings–Network & internet" or pull down the status bar to enter the quick access panel.
2. Tap Wi-Fi, turn on the "Use Wi-Fi" switch, and touch the network to connect to.
3. Enter the password and tap "CONNECT". Check "Show password" to verify input; check "Advanced options" for advanced settings including agent and static IP settings.
4. Check that "Connected" is shown; click the network to view details.

### 2.3 WWAN

Refer to "Instruction on SIM/TF Card installation" for inserting a SIM card. Open "Settings–Network & internet–Mobile network" to set the mobile network with carrier information (data on/off, roaming on/off, access point name, etc.).

DT50 supports a wide range of 4G networks. If WWAN connection fails:

1. Check local network signal intensity.
2. Check whether the SIM card is correctly installed.
3. Check whether the SIM card has any pending bill, is available for surfing, or is damaged.
4. Check whether the access point is correctly set.

### 2.4 Bluetooth

Devices with Bluetooth (printer, access point, other mobile computers) can exchange data. DT50 supports BLE (Bluetooth low energy). To enable Bluetooth:

1. Pull down the status bar and long press the Bluetooth icon, or click "Settings–Connected devices".
2. Tap "Pair new devices" to search nearby Bluetooth devices.
3. Tap "Device name" to change the visible name.
4. Tap the device to connect and enter the PIN code for verifying pairing.

### 2.5 Display

Display settings adjust brightness level, dark theme, wallpaper, auto-rotate screen, screen timeout, and font size.

**Screen rotation settings:** disabled by default. To enable: pull down the status bar and click the screen rotation icon, or check "Settings–Display–Auto-rotate screen". "Keep current orientation" locks the orientation; "orientation of screen rotation" rotates subject to screen content. Rotation is not available for the home screen.

**Sleep settings:** press the power key to turn off the screen or set sleep time. Touch the settings icon → "Display–Screen timeout" → select a sleep time. Press the power key to wake the screen and unlock back to the program interface.

### 2.6 Sound Settings

1. Press the volume key to regulate media volume; slide the volume icon to set the volume.
2. Click the settings icon to open sound settings for media volume, call volume, ringer volume, alarm volume, ringtone, alarm tone, etc.
3. "Settings–Sound" sets media volume, call volume, ringer volume, alarm volume, ringtone, alarm tone, other notification tones, or vibration.

### 2.7 Privacy Settings

1. Open "Settings–Privacy–Permission manager".
2. Click the permission to view or modify (e.g., SMS permission).
3. View applications that have been allowed or denied this permission and click the application to modify.
4. Modify whether the application allows the permission.

### 2.8 Date and Time Settings

When networked, the system automatically updates time and date; users only need to set the time zone. If time is not updated, check whether the device is networked or the WLAN supports the network time protocol (NTP). For manual settings: open "Settings–System–Date & time" → disable "Use network-provided time" → modify time.

### 2.9 Reset Settings

"Restore Device" is a built-in restoration toolkit to initialize the system. Attention: copy and save important files before resetting — restoration erases all user data.

1. Pull down the status bar to open the quick access panel.
2. Tap the settings icon to enter "Settings–System–Advanced–Reset options".
3. Select: Reset Wi-Fi/mobile data network/Bluetooth; Reset app preferences; Erase All Data (factory reset).

### 2.10 About Devices

Open "Settings–About phone" to view: SIM status (carrier, signal strength, IMEI), Device ID (unique identifier), Model (model and SN), IMEI (IMEI1, IMEI2, MEID), Android version, IP address, Wi-Fi MAC address, Bluetooth address.

### 2.11 Scan Settings

Scanning engines may differ in the settings interface; N6603 is taken as an example. For more details, contact Urovo technical staff.

**Floating action button (suspension button):** opens the virtual key scanning tool on the desktop — USCAN — draggable and touchable to simulate the key-scanning function.

**Triggering Mode:**

- **Auto mode (Decode or Time-out):** manually trigger; the laser/supplementary light stays on until decoding completes or scanning times out.
- **Continuous mode (KeyDown start, Again KeyDown stop):** the light does not go out after decoding; decoding sustains.
- **Manual mode (Trigger release or Time-out):** press the scanning key to show the light; release the key and the light goes out; it also goes out after decoding.

**Keystroke output:** check this mode and scanning results are output to the app focus edit box in the foreground by keyboard.

- **Keyboard type** — options include Auto text mode, Soft keyboard, Always Physical keyboard (selection may affect scanning results). Auto text mode: if a focus input box arises in the foreground, it shows all data directly, otherwise it outputs results character by character via keyboard simulation. Soft keyboard: displays all data in the input box via input method editor simulation. Always Physical keyboard: outputs scanning data via physical key event simulation at any interface.
- **Sounds** — notification tone for scanning: None, Instant (default), or Squeak.
- **Vibrate** — check to enable vibrating after scanning.
- **Action key character** — None, Carriage return, Line feed, Tab: adds operational key characters behind scanning results through keyboard simulation (None: no operation; Carriage return: add an enter; Line feed: add an OK operation; Tab: add a Tab key).

**Reader Params:**

- **Coding format:** Chinese code type is UTF-8 by default; must correspond to the 2D code Chinese type, or code garbling may occur.
- **Beam timer:** laser duration for failing in a single scan, range 500–10000 ms (5000 ms default).
- **Linear code type security level:** set the check level to lower the error rate; higher level is more accurate but slower.
- **Exposure Settings–Lights Configuration:** Illum/Aimer — Off, Aimer Only, Illum Only, Alternating, Concurrent. Exposure Mode: auto/on (auto lets the engine regulate exposure; on lets the value be set). Fixed Exposure: 1–900; higher is brighter.

**Basic data formatting:** upon access of bar codes, additional functions may be available: Formatting, Prefix, Suffix, Substitute, Delete Non-printing Control Character, App Identifier, or Code Id Type.

1. Tap "Label Formatting" and select the formatting type.
2. Suffix/Prefix: select the mode and modify the content (e.g., [CR] indicates enter); choose a default type from the drop-down list or define any character.
3. Replace: select the mode and modify the former character and substitute character; the former character is input in ASCII code (e.g., to substitute "1", enter ASCII code value "31").
4. Delete Non-printing Control Character: delete any non-display character.
5. App Identifier: add an app identifier to some bar codes (e.g., Code 128).
6. App Delimited Identifier: "()" is set by default and may be substituted by any other identifier.
7. Code Id Type: None, Aim (add an Aim in front of bar codes), or Symbol (add an identifier in front of bar codes).

### 2.12 ScanWedge Profiles

ScanWedge Profiles allows modifying default scan settings and creating new ones.

1. Click "Default" to modify scan settings for the default application.
2. To create new scan settings and associate apps: (a) click the top-right option and select "New profile"; (b) name the new scan configuration; (c) touch "Associated Apps"; (d) touch "Associate more app"; (e) select the app to associate.

### 2.13 Feature Settings

- **Status bar pull down** — default "on"; if disabled, the status bar function cannot be modified.
- **Virtual key** — default "on"; after disabling, clicking the virtual key at the bottom of the screen does not take effect.
- **Hide navigation bar** — default "off"; select "on" to hide the bottom navigation bar.
- **Input method editor pops up automatically** — default "on"; select "Disable this function" to disable automatic pop-up during code scanning.
- **Pop-up input method** — default "on"; the soft keyboard cannot be popped up after closing.
- **Show scan suspension button** — default "on"; select "Off" if you do not want the button key value output in the application.
- **Automatic call recording** — default "off"; all call recordings are automatically saved locally after turning on, for information traceability.
- **Three-finger screenshots** — default "off"; screenshot by swiping up or down with three fingers simultaneously.
- **Screen pinning** — default "off"; the shortcut key (power + volume +) fixes the current screen; press again to cancel.
- **Default Launcher** — modifies the default desktop; automatically enters the selected desktop.

### 2.14 Remap Key

Remap Key can key-map all physical keys except the power key, giving mapped keys a new key value for customized functions.

1. Open "Settings–Enterprise featured setting–Remap Key" and check "Enable intercept key".
2. Touch "Key Remap".
3. Press the physical key for remapping; the key value and name are shown.
4. Tap KeyCode, Start Application, or Send Broadcast; after finishing, tap "Remap key".
5. View the remapped key list in "Show key remapping list" to manage remapped keys: (a) slide an option left to reset the key to default and clear the mapping value; (b) touch the menu/settings icon at the top right to reset all remapped keys and restore default values.

### 3.3 Enterprise Launcher

Enterprise launcher maximizes enterprise customers' needs for PDA tools, brings a concise enterprise desktop, and restricts access to programs and settings without writing code. It is like a local MDM/MAM with reduced functions and partial Android permissions.

- **User interface:** the launcher used by end users, displaying only available applications. The user interface is fixed, has no long-press trigger, and cannot be uninstalled. Click the menu button in the upper-right to enter the administrator settings interface or view device information; click "Launch" to officially enable the enterprise launcher (users cannot log out).
- **Administrator login:** click the menu key to enter the administrator login interface; the login password is blank by default on first login and can be modified afterwards.
- **Administrator interface:** displays all apps on the device; you can install, uninstall, and view app details. (1) Long press an application to pop up the selection bar (app details, uninstall). (2) Click the menu button to select administrator settings, device information, or exit enterprise mode (returns to the Android launcher).

### 3.4 U Stage

U Stage provides enterprise IT administrators and agents with quick device configuration synchronization.

- **Configure:** before using, the prototype must be configured to working state. Configuration includes system settings (WIFI switch, auto-saved networks, timezone) and scanner settings (scanning handle switch, broadcast configuration, barcode settings).
- **Export:** after the prototype standard configuration is completed, export the configuration scheme via 3 transmission modes:
  1. **Upload to UMS** — upload the current configuration to the UMS Configure Management page for silent batch import.
  2. **Generate barcode** — import the configuration into a barcode for other devices to scan and import.
  3. **Export to local** — generate 2 files: `scanner_system_5.pdf` (contains a configuration barcode to print/transfer) and `scanner_system_5.txt` (uploadable to an MDM platform for remote silent deployment).
- **Import:** after clicking "Import", the scanner lights up (or use the physical scanner key), scan the QR code generated earlier, and the device reboots to complete deployment.

### 3.5 WIFI Diagnosis

Real-time WiFi analysis and troubleshooting tool that accurately locates and solves problems. Main interface shows current signal details (connected WIFI name, signal strength in dBm, frequency band, channel, IP and MAC address) plus a real-time waveform graph of signal intensity.

- **Roaming test:** test packet loss during roaming and observe AP roaming handover.
- **Filter:** filter out only WiFi that meets manually selected conditions.
- **PING test:** test connectivity, packet loss rate, and stability of the current network.
- **Distributed testing:** check WiFi around the device (channel, MAC address) to understand surrounding wireless distribution.
- **Channel analysis:** view interference of each channel across WLAN frequency bands.
- **Expert mode:** set the WiFi roaming threshold, control roaming on/off, and set WLAN and designed channel connection to improve WiFi connection.

### 3.6 Computer Connected Operation

If the Android system driver or Android mobile assistant is installed on the PC, connect the device via the USB cable.

**Developer options:**

1. For first-time debugging, open "Settings–About phone" and click the version number continuously to bring up developer mode.
2. Open "Settings–System–Advanced–Developer options" and enable USB Debugging.
3. Insert the USB cable between the device and PC; a USB enabling popup appears. Tick "Allow this computer for debugging" and select "OK".

**File management by MTP:** connect via USB; pull down the status bar and select "File Transfer" in USB Purpose to transfer media files to Windows (or use an Android file transfer app for Mac, refer to www.android.com/filetransfer). At the PC, open My Computer to access the DT50 device, open the internal storage or SD card folder, and manage media files.

**Picture management by PTP:** connect via USB; select PTP in USB Preference to transfer photos. At the PC, open My Computer → SD card or internal storage folder → manage picture files.

**Charging mode:** connect via USB; select "No data transfer" in USB Preference. In charging mode, data communication is not available.

**Disconnection from PC:** safely unplug at PC before removing the USB cable. Right-click the external device identification in the task bar and select "Unplug DT50 Device", then carefully remove the USB cable.

## Chapter 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting

### 5.1 Maintenance

- Do not scratch the screen. Use a recognized touch pen or plastic-tipped pen; do not use a real pen, pencil, or anything sharp.
- The screen is glass; do not drop or hit DT50.
- Prevent too high or low temperature; do not place on an automotive instrument panel in hot weather; keep away from heat sources.
- Do not use DT50 anywhere dusty, damp, or moist.
- Clean the scanning engine and camera with a soft lens cloth; if the screen gets dirty, clean it with a soft cloth and diluted glass-cleaning solution.

### 5.2 Cleaning

- **Housing:** scrub with a cloth with alcohol, including keys.
- **Display screen:** scrub with alcohol wipes, ensuring no liquid drops near the display edge; immediately dry with a soft, abrasive-free cloth.
- **Camera and scanning window:** scrub with lens wipers or other materials for cleaning optical materials (e.g., glasses).

2. Use accessories approved by the manufacturer and matched with this model. Unapproved power supplies, chargers, or batteries may cause fire, explosion, or other hazards.
