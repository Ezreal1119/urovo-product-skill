### Safety Precautions

- Use only the manufacturer-specified charger. Do not disassemble, impact, crush, or throw into fire. Discontinue use if the battery becomes severely swollen. Do not expose to high temperatures, and do not use after water immersion.
- Do not store the battery in places exceeding 60 °C (140 °F) (e.g., inside vehicles in hot weather under direct sunlight, near radiators, or other heat sources).
- Do not use a battery that is deformed, swollen, or discolored; replace it immediately.
- Do not use a battery that is too hot to handle.
- Do not disassemble, open, crush, bend, deform, puncture, or shred the battery.
- Do not drop the battery or apply mechanical shock or pressure.
- Do not short-circuit the battery or allow metal/conductive objects to touch its terminals.
- Do not throw into fire or expose to high temperatures (microwaves, dryers).
- Do not modify/refurbish, insert foreign objects, submerge in liquid, or expose to fire/explosion hazards.
- Keep the charging area free of debris, flammable materials, or chemicals.
- Store batteries out of the reach of children.
- Follow local disposal regulations; cover terminals with insulating tape before disposal.
- If swallowed, seek medical attention immediately; if leaked, avoid contact and rinse with water.

### Use and Storage

- Configure screen timeout and enable adaptive brightness to reduce usage; disable unnecessary background apps; use Battery Saver and Battery Management; use Airplane Mode when network is unnecessary.
- Lithium-ion capacity declines over time with charge/discharge cycles and environmental factors.
- Charge in an environment between 0 °C (32 °F) and 40 °C (104 °F); avoid extreme temperatures.
- Use the manufacturer-specified charger; incompatible batteries/chargers may cause fire, explosion, or leakage.
- Replace batteries every two years, or if battery health falls below 70%.
- Charge immediately or replace when low; avoid complete depletion.
- Avoid overcharging; disconnect once fully charged.
- For short-term storage, insert a fully charged battery and enable Airplane Mode or power off.
- For battery storage, charge to ~50% at ~25 °C (77 °F).
- For longer storage, remove the battery after charging and store separately in a cool, dry location; check charge level periodically.

### Charge the Battery Before Use

The DT610 has a removable battery; charge for at least 3 hours before use with the supplied adapter and cable or battery dock charger. Using the device while charging increases full-charge time.

1. Connect the charging accessory to the power source.
2. Insert the DT610 into a cradle or attach a USB cable; the Charging/Notification LED is red and turns green when fully charged.

The indicator light is at the top left of the front of the device:

| State | LED | Indication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | — | DT610 is not charging; not inserted correctly; charger/cradle not powered |
| Long red light | Red | DT610 is charging |
| Long green light | Green | Charging complete; remove the power supply promptly |

### Change Battery

Caution: keep hands and parts dry; mating wet components may cause damage not covered by warranty.

**Hot Swap:** the device includes an internal battery that provides limited power to hot-swap the main battery. It powers the display, Wi-Fi, and running applications. When the main battery is removed, it must be replaced with a fully charged battery within 60 seconds; the device will not shut down and maintains its working state.

- The internal battery is user-replaceable.
- Do not install or remove the Micro SD card during a hot swap.
- The internal battery is charged by the main battery; frequent consecutive hot swaps may deplete it.
- Hot-swapping with multiple apps running, or extreme temperatures, may drain the internal battery faster.
- If the internal battery is depleted, a hot-swap attempt will shut the device down and unsaved data may be lost; save important files first.
- Replacing the battery during a call terminates the call.

**Install the battery:**

1. Push from both sides toward the center.
2. Slide both sides to the center to remove the battery cover (hold both rear lock covers to the center, then put the battery outward).
3. Install the battery; the installed battery is flat in the compartment.
4. Place the back cover fully, then push down the lock buckle.

**Replace the battery:** when low, charge or replace with a fully charged spare. Either power off or begin hot swap.

1. Slide the rear cover lock to the center.
2. Slide in the arrow direction to remove the battery cover.
3. Lift the battery upward and install the replacement.
4. Place the back cover fully, then push the lock buckle to the left.

### Check the Battery Status

- **Settings > Battery** — remaining level, usage time, battery usage, battery saver.
- **Battery Management** — remaining capacity, battery health, battery usage, screen time since last full charge, Battery Saver. Replace if health is below 70%.

## Chapter 2 — Using the Device

### Installing SD/SIM Card

1. Remove the battery cover and battery.
2. Insert the TF card / SIM2 card into the card slot.
3. Install the battery.

Note: SIM card is Nano-SIM. Keep the card and hands dry and the card surface clean to avoid poor contact.

### Turn On/Off DT610

**Turn on:** long press the power key; the device vibrates on startup; release and wait. If it does not turn on, charge first or press and hold the power key for over 10 seconds to force reboot.

**Turn off/restart:** press and hold the power button until the shutdown prompt appears; click "Power off". Touch to restart.

### Un-locking the Screen

Use the lock screen to protect data. A pattern, PIN, or password is required to unlock. The screen lock auto-activates when the display turns off.

Setting method: "Settings–Security–Screen lock". Options: None, Swipe, Pattern, PIN, Password, Fingerprint.

- **Pattern screen:** unlock with pattern.
- **Password screen:** unlock with password.
- **PIN screen:** five incorrect entries require a 30-second wait; contact the system administrator if forgotten.
- **Fingerprint screen:** use the fingerprint sensor on the back side, capturing multiple areas of your finger.

### Notification Icons

More notifications; data syncing; upcoming event (non-GMS/GMS); open Wi-Fi; song playing; sign-in/sync problem; uploading/downloading; VPN; internal storage check; call in progress/voice messages/on hold/missed; headset connected.

### Quick Access Panel

Notification panel (drag down status bar); quick setting panel; device settings; edit quick settings panel.

### Edit Quick Settings Panel

Hold and drag to rearrange tiles and change icon order.

### Remove the Application from the Home Screen

Note: removing an app also removes related user data and configuration files. Drag the icon into the "remove" area and release.

### Create a Folder

Go to Home Screen; touch and hold an app icon; drag and stack on another icon; lift and release. Name the folder via the title area.

Hold down the home screen until the menu appears → "Widgets"; scroll, hold, position, release.

## Chapter 4 — Use the Scan Engine

### About the Image Engine

The DT610 supports 360° barcode scanning with an aiming beam. Enable only the needed barcode symbologies (Settings > Enterprise featured setting > Scanner Settings, or ScanDemo > Setting) to improve scanning speed.

1. Point the scanner window toward the barcode; angle slightly to avoid reflections.
2. Press and hold either (or both) scan buttons to activate the aiming beam.
3. A beep indicates successful reading.

### Scanner Settings

Note: the OCR settings option appears only when a license is installed.

- Suspension button (USCAN virtual key).
- Supports changing the triggered-scanning form (e.g., continuous scanning).
- Multi-barcode parameters.
- Output mode: Intent, Keystroke, Keystroke + Intent.
- Additional functions: Formatting, Prefix, Suffix, Substitute, Delete Non-printing Control Character, App Identifier, Code Id Type.
- Characters delay, key event delay.

### ScanWedge Profiles

ScanWedge sends scanned data to an app as keypad input. Click "Default" to modify default settings; create new profiles and associate apps: (a) top-right → "New profile"; (b) name; (c) "Associated Apps"; (d) "Associate more app"; (e) select the app.

### Floating Action Button

Suspension button: opens the virtual key scanning tool USCAN.

### Triggering Mode

- **Decode or Time-out:** light stays on until decoding completes or times out.
- **Hands-free continuous:** start continuous scanning; press the scan key again to stop.
- **Trigger release or Time-out:** release the key to stop; also stops after decoding.
- **Continuous trigger mode:** targeting beam only while the scan button is pressed.
- **Trigger aiming & loose for scanning:** press to start; scanning ends once the barcode is read.

### Keystroke Output

- **Keyboard type:** Auto text mode, Soft keyboard, Always Physical keyboard.
- **Sounds:** None, Instant (default), Squeak.
- **Vibrate:** vibrate after scanning.
- **Action key character:** None, Carriage return, Line feed, Tab.

### Reader Params

- **Coding format:** UTF-8 by default; must match the 2D code type.
- **Beam timer:** 500–10000 ms (5000 ms default).
- **Linear code type security level:** higher is more accurate but slower.
- **Exposure Settings–Lights Configuration:** Illum/Aimer (Off, Aimer Only, Illum Only, Alternating, Concurrent); Exposure Mode auto/on; Fixed Exposure 1–900.

### Basic Data Formatting

Formatting, Prefix, Suffix, Substitute, Delete Non-printing Control Character, App Identifier, Code Id Type.

1. Tap "Label Formatting" and select the type.
2. Suffix/Prefix (e.g., [CR] = enter).
3. Replace (ASCII code, e.g., "1" → "31").
4. Delete Non-printing Control Character.
5. App Identifier (e.g., Code 128).
6. App Delimited Identifier ("()" default).
7. Code Id Type (None, Aim, Symbol).

## Chapter 5 — Use the RFID

The DT610 next-generation RFID module leverages cutting-edge radio frequency technology:

- RFID tag maximum read range of **1.5 m (5 ft)**.
- RFID read speed of **50 tags per second**.
- RFID is **disabled by default**, enabled/disabled via RFID SDK APIs.
- RFID and WWAN can co-exist.

### Optimal Read Range

Adjust positioning; larger tags require slightly greater separation; high-density tags perform better with closer proximity.

### Antenna Orientation

Angle the reader antenna to prevent signal reflection; a 15–30° tilt often improves detection; extreme angles may weaken signal strength; metal surfaces and liquids may require adjusted positioning.

### Performance Tips

Slow, steady motions improve read rates; affix tags to flat, non-metallic surfaces; distance the reader from other RF sources (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth) for high-priority scanning. Avoid placing hands and fingers on the RFID antenna.

## Chapter 8 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting

### Maintenance Equipment

- Do not scratch the screen; the screen is glass — do not drop or subject to strong impact.
- Protect from temperature extremes.
- Do not use anywhere dusty, damp, or wet.
- Clean with a soft lens cloth / diluted window-cleaning solution.

**Long-term storage:** battery at least 50% charge, stored at -40–70 °C.

### Cleaning Equipment

Materials: alcohol wipes, lens tissue, cotton-tipped applicators, isopropyl alcohol, compressed air.

- Housing: alcohol wipes including buttons.
- Display: alcohol wipes, avoid liquid pooling, dry with a soft non-abrasive cloth.
- Camera/exit window: lens tissue.
- Connector cleaning: dip applicator in alcohol, rub, repeat ≥3 times, dry applicator, compressed air ½ inch away.
- Cradle connectors: remove DC power; clean pins; all sides; compressed air; remove lint; air dry 10–30 minutes before powering.

### Troubleshooting

| Problem | Reason | Solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press the power key but DT610 does not work | Battery not charged / system crashes / battery broken | Charge / reset / replace battery |
| The battery is not charged | Battery temperature exceptional | Contact technical support |
| Wi-Fi data transfer fails | Wi-Fi not enabled / out of range | Enable Wi-Fi / move closer |
| Bluetooth data transfer fails | Bluetooth not enabled / out of range | Enable Bluetooth / move within 10 m (32.8 ft) |
| No sound | Volume too low or off | Adjust volume |
| Screen turns off | Inactive state / battery depleted | Set duration 15 s–30 min / charge |
| Button/icon fails to trigger | Device not responding | Reset |
| Storage full | Too many files / apps | Delete memos, use SD card / uninstall apps |
| Fails to decode barcode | Barcode unreadable / wrong distance / wrong type / low battery | Check barcode / reposition / set barcode type / check battery |
| Cannot unlock | Incorrect password | After eight attempts, enter a code; contact administrator |
| Cannot find Bluetooth devices | Too far / not turned on / not discoverable | Move within 10 m / turn on / set discoverable |

### About a Factory Reset

Erases all information in internal shared storage and the IPSM card storage. Back up first. eSIM can be kept or erased (not available on all models/regions). If a Google account is added, have the username/password, and reset 24 hours after a recent password change.
