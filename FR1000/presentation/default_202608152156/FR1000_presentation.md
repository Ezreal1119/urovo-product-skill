## Slide 1 — Title

**Title:** FR1000 — Intelligent Fixed UHF RFID Reader

*Visual:* Full-frame cinematic render of the FR1000 reader on a sci-fi bridge with glowing blue waves. The reader is a long black industrial housing with **four front-mounted SMA antenna ports** and a side power connector.

## Slide 2 — Unparalleled Computing Power

- Running on the **Octa-core 1.6 GHz high-performance CPU**, FR1000 is equipped with strong processing power.
- It can simultaneously manage multiple processes.
- Meanwhile, the **Android 10.0** operating system brings superior performance and efficiency.

*Visual:* Holographic CPU chip render with a security shield icon overlay. Glowing blue translucent CPU badge.

---

## Slide 3 — Fast Reading RFID Tags in One Second

- The **Self-Jammer Cancellation (SJC) technology** provides interference suppression and echo cancellation, enabling the FR1000 to read with higher read rate. It can capture RFID tags in batches (**max. 700+ RFID tags/sec**).

*Visual:* Warehouse boxes stacked in background; white square **6 dBi flat-panel antenna** rendered in front with concentric blue radar rings emanating outward; black FR1000 reader also visible.

---

## Slide 4 — Enhanced Security

- The FR1000 enables you to **manually change IPv6 addresses** and proactively monitor the network, improving security and protecting your privacy.

*Visual:* Abstract network-graph render with blue nodes and a glowing data-trail line.

---

## Slide 5 — Flexible Deployment Without Additional Power Socket

- The FR1000 supports **PoE+ (802.3at 25.5W)** power supply mode. It can be flexibly deployed in the desired position without additional power sockets, greatly reducing the time and deployment costs.

*Visual:* Large "POE+" graphic with the FR1000 reader rotated to show its **rear I/O panel** (DC jack, USB ports, Ethernet RJ45).

---

## Slide 6 — Rugged and Durable Design for Tough Environments

- The FR1000 made of **cast aluminum housing** has superior thermal management which ensures stable performance and output, delivering the durability you need to ensure uptime — even in damp, dusty work areas, extreme heat or subzero temperatures.

*Visual:* The reader rendered from below with **downward blue cooling arrows** fanning out — visualizing heat dissipation.

---

## Slide 7 — Great Compatibility And Improved Accuracy

- **Eight external SMA antenna ports** improve the accuracy and increase the reading range.
- The independent **anti-interference technology of 4G Main & AUX antenna, WiFi/Bluetooth antenna, and GPS antenna** significantly improves the communication efficiency, and enables you to extend RFID to the field.

*Visual:* Two white square antennas with concentric blue rings, plus a black multi-pin connector cable and a finned black heat-sink element. Heavy use of concentric-circle graphics emphasizing range/accuracy.

---

## Slide 8 — Efficient Management Tools

- Equipped with integrated **HDMI, RJ45, RS232, GPIO** and other interfaces, FR1000 can centrally manage various tools in a variety of RFID applications.

*Visual:* Rear-panel close-up of the FR1000 showing, left to right: DC power jack, USB Type-A, HDMI, RJ45 Ethernet, RS232 (DB9), USB Type-A — six ports in a row.

---

## Slide 9 — Adaptable to Various Industry Scenarios

- FR1000 applies to various industry scenarios, such as **retail, jewelry, banking, archives, library, and medical care**.

*Visual:* Four vertical photos side by side — clothing store apparel rack, jewelry store display with necklaces, pearl/jewelry counter, pharmacist reaching for a medication bottle on pharmacy shelves.
