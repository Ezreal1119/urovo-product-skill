## 4. Disassembly steps

### 1、Rear shell disassembly (PDF pages 4–5 / printed 2–3)

First, take out the battery and back cover, and then use a screwdriver to remove the **8 black Phillips screws** from the rear shell of the fuselage.

Open the lid of the printer paper deck cover and take out the printer roller. Use a screwdriver to remove the **2 black Phillips screws** and **2 silver Phillips screws** inside, then use disassembly lamella remove the back shell.

**Visual:** rear of the white POS, **8 blue-circled** perimeter screws; paper-deck open with roller out; additional screws inside the printer bay; rear shell lifted.

### 2、Paper deck cover disassembly (PDF pages 5–6 / printed 3–4)

First, take the front shell assembly and open the paper tray cover. Gently pry the two end clips of the paper bin cover outward and remove the cover. Hold the paper bin cover securely from below with your right hand, then use the thumbs of both hands to push it leftwards, separating the paper bin cover from the decorative trim.

**(Note: If you do not need to replace the NFC antenna, paper bin cover, and other related materials, please skip this step to perform the next step)**

**Visual:** paper-bin cover and decorative trim separated; red arrows **left thumb presses against here and pushes outward** / **right thumb presses against here and pushes outward**.

### 3、Speaker bracket disassembly (PDF pages 6–7 / printed 4–5)

First, use a screwdriver to remove the **two black screws** securing the speaker bracket. Then disconnect the speaker and RTC battery connector from the mainboard. Next, detach the POGOPIN_FPC (First, raise the connector's snap fasteners). Finally, remove the speaker bracket assembly.

Then remove the speaker, spare battery, POGOPIN_FPC from the speaker bracket **(Note: If you do not need to replace the speaker, spare battery, POGOPIN_FPC, and antenna related materials, this step does not need to be performed)**.

**Visual:** speaker-bracket area; RTC coin cell; POGOPIN FPC; speaker and spare battery removed onto the bench.

### 4、Camera disassembly (PDF pages 7–8 / printed 5–6)

Lift the buckle on the printer connector up to separate the printer FPC. Separate the connector of the NFC probe from the motherboard interface and the camera from the motherboard.

Remove the camera from the printer stand with tweezers assistance.

**Visual:** printer FPC unlatched; camera lifted from the printer stand with tweezers.

### 5、Mainboard disassembly (PDF page 8 / printed 6)

Remove **2 silver motherboard screws** with a screwdriver. Then press the head of the device down with your left hand, and gently take it out with your right hand while pinching the motherboard and gently taking it out, and gently folding the motherboard along the edge of the battery compartment. Then separate the screen FPC from the motherboard and take out the motherboard.

**Visual:** two motherboard screws; red box **Press here** at the head of the device; board folded away from the battery well; screen FPC separated.

### 6、Magnetic head disassembly (PDF page 9 / printed 7)

First, remove the zebra strip and antenna coaxial wire from the motherboard. Then separate the head FPC and conductive cloth from the motherboard, remove the **2 silver magnetic head bracket screws**, and then remove the head assembly. Remove **1 silvery-white screw** from the head holder, remove the head and separate the conductive cloth.

**Visual:** MSR / magnetic-head assembly at the top of the chassis; zebra strip and coaxial aside; head and conductive cloth separated.

### 7、Printer disassembly (PDF pages 10–11 / printed 8–9)

Comb out the NFC probe connection cable from the card slot on the printer stand, and remove the camera conductive cloth from the printer stand. Then use a screwdriver to remove the **4 black Phillips screws** on the printer stand, then take out the printer holder assembly.

Use tweezers to lift the NFC probe connector from the face housing and take it out. Remove the **2 black Phillips screws** from the printer and remove the printer.

**Visual:** printer stand with four screws; NFC probe lifted from the face housing; printer mechanism removed.

## 6. Repair of common faults (PDF pages 15–20 / printed 13–18)

Heading numbers in this chapter stay **5.x** in the source.

### 5.1 Trigger relevant issues

① After the device is switched on, the yellow trigger interface is prompted, and the device enters the homepage after the trigger is solved normally. After the device is switched off and the battery is plugged in again and switched on, it is still in the yellow trigger interface: the RTC battery is dead, replace it with a new RTC battery.

② After the device is switched on, the red trigger interface is prompted, for details, please refer to the **Trigger Maintenance Guide**.

### 5.2 Battery charging relevant issues: no charging

① Check whether the battery, power adapter, and data cable are well connected (no looseness).  
② Replace the power adapter data cable and battery access device that function normally and check whether it is charging normally.  
③ Check whether the USB port is damaged, and replace the USB port if it is damaged.  
④ None of the above problems exist, replace the motherboard.

### 5.3 Equipment start-up relevant issues

**1、No display on the screen**

① Check if the battery is well connected (no looseness) and if the battery is dead, replace the battery with a normal one and check again.  
② Check whether the LCD_FPC is installed, or connected correctly(no looseness), to confirm that there is no problem with the installation before testing.  
③ Screen replacement.

**2、Screen flashes but does not go to main page**

① Check whether the battery is well connected (no looseness), whether the battery is insufficiently charged, if there is a problem with the battery replace the battery with a good one and check.  
② Disassemble the machine to check if LCD_FPC is well connected.  
③ Screen replacement.

### 5.4 ICC relevant issues: IC card information cannot be read

① Re-insert the IC card and check whether the IC card is inserted in place (whether the card slot is deformed or whether there is any foreign object in the card slot).  
② Replacement of IC card test.  
③ Whether the software version is correct.  
④ None of the above problems exist, replace the motherboard.

### 5.5 PICC relevant issues

① No card reading: check whether the NFC antenna gold finger on the paper bin cover is damaged (use a multimeter to measure whether the two points are connected, if not, replace face shell）；Check if the NFC probe cable is well connected or damaged.  
② Read the card but no sound from the speaker: check if the speaker is installed or damaged.

### 5.6 MAG relevant issues: Failure to swipe magnetic stripe card

① Whether there is an error in the swiping operation, retry swiping the card according to the correct method.  
② Check if the head is mounted, or connected correctly(no looseness), or replace the head with a new one.  
③ Check whether the brush slot of the magnetic head of the face shell is deformed and whether there is any foreign matter in the slot.

### 5.7 Printer relevant issues

**1、Printing does not go paper:** check whether the printer is loaded, check the print paper, printer rollers are loaded.

**2、print failure (that is, the printer does not work):**

① Whether the printing paper is complete and not missing paper.  
② Check whether the printer FPC is well connected to the connector.  
③ Replacement of normal printer.

**3、Printing stutters, unclear fonts, dull colors:**

① Check the printer roller gear and printer bracket gear for damage, and check for foreign objects in the printer gear slot.  
② Check whether the paper compartment cover is deformed (remove the printer roller from the paper compartment cover and install it on the printer bracket to test whether it can print normally, if it prints normally, it means that the paper compartment cover is deformed, replace the paper compartment cover).  
③ SE is whether the software is correct (whether the upgrade is missed) and whether its version number corresponds (the version number of SE will affect the printing results).  
④ Replacement of printers.

### 5.8 Display relevant issues

**1. Screen distortion, blank screen, black screen:**

① Please restart the device.  
② Restore factory settings or reinstall the software.  
③ Replace the front shell assembly.

**2. Touchscreen misalignment or unresponsive touch:**

① Inspect the screen for foreign objects and clean it.  
② Attempt to power off and restart, or reinstall the software.  
③ Replace the front shell assembly.

**3. No response upon powering on:**

① First, reinstall the operating system software.  
② Disassemble the device to check whether the LCD_FPC is installed correctly.  
③ Replace the display panel assembly.

### 5.9 PSAM relevant issues

① Check if the PSAM card is damaged and cannot be read, change a PSAM card to test.  
② Check whether the slot shrapnel is deformed, whether the card holder is properly welded, reweld or replace the card holder.  
③ Update software OS and SE.

### 5.10 SIM relevant issues

**1、The card inspection test failed**

① Check whether the SIM card is damaged, and test with another SIM card.  
② Check whether the slot shrapnel is deformed, whether the card holder is properly welded, re-welding or replacement of the card holder.

**2、No SIM card signal display**

① Check whether the antenna is installed.  
② Whether the SIM card slot is soldered well.  
③ If the SIM card is damaged, replace the SIM card.

### 5.11 TF card relevant issues

① Check if the SD card is damaged and cannot be read, change a TF card for testing.  
② Check whether the slot shrapnel is deformed, whether the card holder is properly welded, reweld or replace the card holder.

### 5.12 Rear camera relevant issues: splash screen or error reporting

① Check if the camera is installed or if the camera FPC is connected correctly （(no looseness)）.  
② Replacement of camera.

### 5.13 Speaker relevant issues

no sound output, please check whether the speaker is installed, whether the wiring and interface are damaged, and replace the speaker or the interface base.
