## Slide 2 — Outstanding Performance

Equipped with the advanced **Impinj E710** chip, it can achieve rapid read rates of up to **1300+ tags per second**. Its excellent performance allows precise reading of fast-moving RFID tags with an accuracy rate of **99.7%**.

*Visual:* Conveyor belt with stacked cardboard boxes on a production line; a pole-mounted white antenna on the right projects concentric cyan radar arcs over the boxes.

*Footnote (from slide):* "The conveyor belt moves at a speed of 2.5 m/s. Unless otherwise noted, the data are from Urovo's laboratory, for reference only."

## Slide 4 — Extensive Interfaces, Flexible Transmission

Seamlessly integrating **RS232 and RJ45 interfaces** while offering a variety of communication options including **RS485, Wiegand, GPIO, and USB2.0**, it delivers unmatched compatibility, effortlessly adapting to any application scenario.

*Visual:* Interface diagram with two labeled groups above a render of the white FR7000 unit:
- **Standard:** RJ45 (Ethernet), USB2.0
- **Standard:** Power Supply, RS232 (DB9)
- **Optional:** RS485, Wiegand, GPIO Input, GPIO Output
All cable runs route down into the unit's two front-mounted SMA connectors.

## Slide 5 — Versatile Applications

Optional **6 dBi and 9 dBi circular polarized antennas with adjustable output power**, provides two different read ranges of **7 meters (6 dBi) and 12 meters (9 dBi)** for a wide range of industry applications.

*Visual:* Two dashboard panels:
- **Output Power** — 0–33 dBM Adjustable slider, 0 dBm to 33 dBm
- **Reading Range** — 6 dBi = 7 meters, 9 dBi = 12 meters (cone-shaped range visualization)

*Footnote:* "Unless otherwise noted, the data are from Urovo's laboratory, for reference only."
