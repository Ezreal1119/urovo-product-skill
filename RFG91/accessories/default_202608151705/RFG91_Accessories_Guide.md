# RFG91 Accessories Guide

> **Canonical original:** `RFG91/accessories/default_202608151705/RFG91_Accessories_Guide.pdf`
> **Language:** English
> **Scope:** Accessories for the RFG91 Handheld UHF RFID Sled.

## Overview

The RFG91 is a high-performance UHF RFID sled that transforms smartphones and mobile computers into professional-grade RFID readers. Powered by the Impinj E710 chip and supporting Impinj Gen2X, it enables fast data capture for cycle counting, asset tracking, and inventory management.

## Standard

### Adapter (5V/2A)

- Input: 100–240 Vac 50/60 Hz 0.5 A
- Output: 5 V/2 A, 10 W
- Adapter type varies by region

### USB Cable

- Supports charging and data transferring
- Port: USB Type-C

### Battery

- **Standard battery:** removable 3.6 V, 4900 mAh; battery indicator supports smart battery management
- **Extended capacity battery:** removable 3.6 V, 6700 mAh; battery indicator supports smart battery management

## Optional — Charging Cradles

### Single Charging Cradle

- Charges 1 RFG91 device, or 1 RFG91 device + 1 DT610 with rugged case / DT610 Pro without rugged case
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Power supply and cord included: AC input 100–240 V, DC output 12 V/2 A, 24 W

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle

- Charges up to 5 RFG91 devices, or 5 RFG91 + 5 DT610 (rugged case) / DT610 Pro (without case)
- LED charging status indicator; DC jack
- AC input 100–240 V, DC output 24 V/7.5 A, 180 W

### 2-in-1 Battery & Device Charging Cradle (5-Slot)

- Charges up to 4 RFG91 devices (or 4 RFG91 + 4 DT610 rugged / DT610 Pro) and 4 spare RFG91 standard or extended batteries
- LED charging status indicator; DC jack
- AC input 100–240 V, DC output 24 V/7.5 A, 180 W

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle (3 devices + batteries)

- Charges up to 3 RFG91 devices (or 3 RFG91 + 3 DT610 rugged / DT610 Pro) and 4 spare RFG91 batteries and 4 spare DT610/DT610 Pro batteries
- LED charging status indicator; DC jack
- AC input 100–240 V, DC output 24 V/7.5 A, 180 W

### 4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle

- Charges RFG91 standard battery (4900 mAh) or extended capacity battery (6700 mAh)
- LED charging status indicator; DC jack
- AC input 100–240 V, DC output 12 V/5 A, 60 W

## Optional — Carrying & Operation Accessories

- **Magnetic Universal Bluetooth Adaptor Mount:** for PDA or third-party smartphones; compatible with devices with a flat, smooth back.
- **Universal Bluetooth Adaptor Mount:** for PDA or third-party smartphones; compatible with devices 69–79 mm wide and under 12 mm thick.
- **DT610 Adaptor Mount:** pogo-pin connectivity to DT610 with rugged case.
- **DT610 Pro Adaptor Mount:** pogo-pin connectivity to DT610 Pro without rugged case.
- **Wrist Strip:** black thread-through nylon lanyard with reinforced tips.
