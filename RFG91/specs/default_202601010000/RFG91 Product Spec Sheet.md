### Meet the RFG91

Urovo RFG91 is a high-performance UHF RFID sled that transforms smartphones and mobile computers into professional-grade RFID readers. Powered by the Impinj E710 chip and supporting Impinj Gen2X, it delivers fast and accurate data capture for applications such as cycle counting, asset tracking, and inventory management.

Weighing just 390 g, the RFG91 features a lightweight, ergonomic design for efficient mobile use, with seamless connectivity across PDAs, smartphones, and computers. The RFG91 Series offers four flexible form factors — Standard, Magnetic, POGO PIN, and Universal — supporting multi-platform compatibility (Android, iOS, Windows) and fast pairing via NFC, Bluetooth, and QR codes.

With versatile mounting options and high-performance RFID capabilities — up to 1300+ tags/sec and 15 m+ read range — it provides a scalable and efficient solution for modern mobile data collection.

It is ideally suited for industries such as retail, warehousing and logistics, manufacturing, healthcare, and asset-intensive enterprises, enabling faster, more accurate, and more efficient field data operations.

## Page 2 — Product Highlights (8 cards)

### Professional and Versatile Data Collection

All-in-one RFID reading and barcode scanning, three data collection modes, one-click to switch, transforming your phone into a professional data collection device.

### Outstanding RFID Performance

Equipped with the Impinj E710 chip and supporting Impinj Gen2X, it delivers a faster RFID reading rate of 1300+ tags per second and a longer reading range of over 15 meters, enabling fast and accurate inventory and asset tracking. Ideal for large-scale cycle counting scenarios such as high-bay warehouses, apparel distribution centers, and e-commerce logistics hubs.

### Versatile Compatibility

The RFG91 seamlessly connects with devices running Android, iOS, Windows, and more. It supports three fast pairing modes — NFC tap-to-pair, Bluetooth search, and barcode scanning — ensuring quick and effortless setup.

### Multiple Versions for Flexibility

The RFG91 Series offers four versatile form factors to adapt to diverse field scenarios.

- **Standard** — RFID + barcode scanning with quick NFC / Bluetooth / QR pairing.
- **Magnetic** — Strong magnetic mount for fast, tool-free attach/detach.
- **POGO PIN** — RFG91 with DT610 extends RFID from short to long range, with 1,300+ tags/sec and 15 m+ range.
- **Universal** — Adjustable telescopic design for wide device compatibility.

Efficient. Versatile. Built for the field.

### Lightweight & Ergonomic

Weighing just 390 g, it is designed for all users. The ergonomic design ensures comfort during extended use, reducing physical fatigue by 35% during long shifts and minimizing errors caused by worker fatigue.

### Industrial-Grade Durability

Built with industrial-grade durability, the RFG91 is IP54 rated for dust and water resistance (upgradable to IP67) and designed to withstand 1.5 m drop resistance, 0.5 m tumble tests, 1 million scan button presses, and 10 million handle trigger operations. It is well-suited for harsh environments such as dusty or humid warehouses, outdoor logistics, and sorting operations, ensuring reliable performance in demanding field conditions.

### Extended Battery Life

The RFG91 features flexible battery options, including a standard 4900 mAh removable battery and an optional 6700 mAh high-capacity battery, supporting up to 8-12 hours of continuous operation. Comes with 1-slot cradle and 4-slot cradle which enable you to charge the RFG91 by itself, the mobile computer by itself, and both simultaneously.

### AI-Powered Smart Battery Management Featuring Secure Battery Locking Foot

Equipped with AI-powered battery management for real-time monitoring, improved lifespan, and enhanced safety, combined with a low power consumption design for extended field use. A secure battery locking system ensures stable and reliable attachment during operation, delivering consistent and efficient power support for demanding mobile and industrial applications.

### RFIDWedge

RFIDWedge is a no-code RFID input tool that delivers tag data into any application without integration, enabling fast deployment and flexible data control.
