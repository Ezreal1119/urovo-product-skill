## Page 1 - Positioning and Product Features

**The shelf-side information hub for faster retail decisions.**

### Tablet-Like Design. Intuitive Interaction.

An ultra-slim form factor and a 10.1-inch HD touchscreen show live prices, active promotions, and applicable membership pricing on one screen, supporting a complete shelf-side purchase decision.

### Ergonomic Scan Angle. Instant Feedback.

A 30-degree inward-tilted scan window supports comfortable shelf-side scanning and faster price checks. An LED status light and 105 dB speaker provide visual and audible confirmation.

### Robust Performance. Peak Readiness.

Android 13, a quad-core 2.0 GHz processor, and 3 GB RAM plus 32 GB storage are intended to keep interactions responsive and stable during peak hours.

### Multi-Modal Recognition. Broader Uses.

Built-in NFC supports membership-card reading. The 8 MP autofocus camera enables passwordless staff login. Optional UHF RFID is intended to accelerate multi-item lookup in high-SKU retail environments.

### Smart Sensing. Ready When Shoppers Approach.

A built-in presence sensor wakes the screen automatically for touch-free self-service. When idle, U100 can display custom promotional content for in-aisle retail-media use.

### Flexible Deployment. Direct Integration.

Quick-hitch wall mounting and countertop installation support different store formats. VESA compatibility, integrated PoE, and multiple connectivity options simplify integration with existing retail systems.

### Visual Evidence

- The product rendering shows a landscape 10.1-inch terminal with a top-mounted camera/sensor module, a central inward-tilted scan window, an LED status strip, a UHF reading area on the lower left, and an NFC/contactless area on the lower right.
- The in-store illustration shows U100 wall-mounted at shelf height. Its sample UI reads **“Tap or Scan to Discover Price”** and presents **RFID Tag**, **QR Code**, and **Member Card** as shopper interaction modes.
