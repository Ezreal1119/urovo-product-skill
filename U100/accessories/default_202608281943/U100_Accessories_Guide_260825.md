## Page 1 - Product Context and Standard Accessories

UROVO U100 is described as a shelf-side self-service terminal for high-SKU retail. Its mounting and power options support wall-mounted and countertop installations and are intended to create clean, accessible self-service points throughout a store.

The page shows U100 mounted in a retail aisle while a shopper presents a packaged product to the inward-facing scan window. The device rendering also shows the lower UHF and contactless/NFC interaction areas.

### Standard Adapter

**DC power adapter (12 V / 2 A)**

- Provides external power for U100.
- Output: 12 V / 2 A.
- Intended for reliable daily operation.
- Product image shows a wall adapter with attached DC cable and barrel connector.

### Standard Mounting Accessory

**Quick-hitch mounting bracket**

- Secure quick-hitch mounting structure.
- Complete mounting screw kit included.
- Safety-screw hole helps reduce accidental device release.
- Intended for wall-mounted U100 installations.
- Product image shows a square black mounting plate with four corner mounting holes, two upper retaining posts, and a side/bottom safety feature.

## Page 2 - Optional Mounting Accessory

### Height-Adjustable 360-Degree Rotating Desk Clamp Stand

- Height-adjustable design for stable installation.
- 360-degree rotation at the arm joint and mounting head.
- Supported VESA patterns: 50 × 50 mm, 75 × 75 mm, and 100 × 100 mm.
- Pole height: 400 mm.
- Arm length: 115 mm.
- Pole diameter: 28 mm.
- Desk-clamp range: 15 to 90 mm.

The product image shows a vertical pole clamped to a desk edge, an articulated horizontal arm, and a square VESA mounting plate.
