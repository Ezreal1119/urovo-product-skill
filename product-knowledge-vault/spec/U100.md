## Positioning and Features

U100 is a shelf-side retail price checker designed to show live prices, promotions, and membership pricing. It combines a 30-degree inward-tilted 1D/2D scan window with LED and 105 dB audible feedback. Built-in NFC supports membership-card reading, the 8 MP autofocus camera can support passwordless staff login, and the optional adjustable short-range UHF reader defaults to a 20 cm read range. A 1 m presence sensor can wake the screen when a shopper approaches; the device can show promotional content while idle.
