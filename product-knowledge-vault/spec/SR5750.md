## Overview

The SR5750 is a 70 g Android 13 wearable computer for warehouse, logistics, and retail workflows. It combines a 2.1-inch AMOLED touchscreen, professional 1D/2D scanning, a 13 MP PDAF camera, NFC, Wi-Fi 6, and Bluetooth 5.3 in an IP67, 1.8 m drop-resistant form factor.
