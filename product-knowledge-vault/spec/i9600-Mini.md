## Overview

Phone-style MiniPOS (177 × 79.8 × 17 mm, 298 g) with a 6.745" 720×1600 display, removable 5000 mAh battery, and MSR / IC / contactless payment. No integrated receipt printer and no customer-facing second display on this specification — unlike [[spec/i9600|i9600]].
