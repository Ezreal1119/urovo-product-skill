## User Guide Operational Facts

> **Source**: `U2S/user_guide/default_202608151537/U2S (EN)-User Guide_20250703.pdf` (document date: 2025-07-03)

| Topic | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Battery model | HBLU2 removable main battery |
| First charge | At least 3 hours with the supplied adapter and cable |
| Hot swap | Internal battery maintains the display, Wi-Fi, and running applications; install a fully charged main battery within 60 seconds |
| Battery health | Consider replacement below 70% health or at about two years |
| Storage card | Rear Micro SD/TF slot; do not install or remove the card during hot swap |
| Charging interface | Magnetic pogo-pin connector; red LED while charging and green when complete |
| Glove operation | Gloves Mode under Enterprise featured setting; source specifies gloves below 2 mm |
| Enterprise provisioning | U Stage can upload a configuration to UMS, export a scannable barcode, or save the configuration locally |
| Warranty | Device 12 months and accessories 3 months under the stated standard policy, subject to the sales agreement |

The guide also covers Wi-Fi/hotspot, Bluetooth, NFC, certificate installation, key remapping, cleaning, factory reset, and detailed troubleshooting. Its screenshots and available functions are explicitly described as samples that may vary by model and OS. The Battery Management screenshot's `5430 mAh` present-capacity value is therefore sample UI data, not a U2S battery specification.
