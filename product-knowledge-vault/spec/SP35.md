## Data Capture and Retail Features

- Standard-range 1D/2D scanner; FlexRange optional.
- 16 MP autofocus rear camera with PDAF, flash, and torch.
- Front-facing NFC with SoftPOS support.
- Optional UWB for navigation and device tracking.
- Optional UHF RFID is **not** listed on the 2026-09-02 specification or New Product Announcement. The Battlecard still says RFID (Optional); see [[products/SP35|SP35 Source Hub]].
- Configurable PTT button for customer-to-staff assistance.

## Durability

- 1.5 m drops to tile over concrete across -10°C to 50°C, per MIL-STD-810H.
- 1000 × 0.5 m tumbles at room temperature.
- ESD: ±15 kV air, ±8 kV direct, ±8 kV indirect.

The current specification page 4 visibly prints **-10°C to 50°C / 14°F to 122°F**. The earlier missing-minus rendering note is resolved for this revision.
