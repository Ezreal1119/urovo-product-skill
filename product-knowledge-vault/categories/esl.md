# Electronic Shelf Labels

2.4 GHz active e-paper price tags plus a matching F24A7 base station and cloud/local management software.

## Overview

UROVO ESL replaces paper shelf labels with reusable e-paper tags that show price, promo, barcode/QR, and (on larger sizes) graphics. Tags talk to a ceiling/wall base station over a private 2.4 GHz protocol, or to a phone app (NFC and/or Bluetooth, depending on the source). A 1.54-inch size appears in the English overview; its spec is not yet synchronized.

## Key Technologies

- E-paper, 2/3/4 color (black/white/red/yellow on 4-color SKUs)
- Bluetooth 5.0 private protocol on ET0213/ET0420; T2664 lists GFSK private 2.4 GHz
- Optional NFC, temperature and battery sampling, RGB LED blink, 8 stored pages (ET0213/ET0420)
- F24A7: external omni/directional antenna, self-developed 2.4 GHz ESL protocol, Ethernet, optional WIFI listed only in the PDF advantages paragraph

Hard-spec conflicts (15-year vs ≥7-year battery, IP68 vs IP65 default, 100 m vs 30 m) are recorded on [[products/ESL|the ESL Hub]].
