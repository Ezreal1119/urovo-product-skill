## Product Highlights

**6.745" 720×1600** · **17 mm / 298 g** · **Android 13** · **5000 mAh removable** · **PCI & EMV** · **no onboard printer**

## Key Features

### MiniPOS form
177 × 79.8 × 17 mm, 298 g. Single 6.745-inch display; no customer-facing second screen and no thermal printer on the dedicated specification.

### Payments
Magnetic stripe, IC, and contactless. Contactless notification LEDs (blue / yellow / green / red).

### Optional capture
Optional professional scan engine; front camera optional (default none); rear 5 MP AF, 16 MP optional.

## Key Differentiator

Same 6.745" class display as i9600, without the dual-screen printer chassis.
