## Product Highlights

**Qualcomm Q-6690** · **Android 15→20** · **5-inch HD** · **Wi-Fi 6E** · **BT 5.3** · **UWB** · **SoftPOS**

## Key Features

- Self-shopping support from product search and scanning through checkout.
- UWB-powered product navigation and device tracking for up to 48 hours after power-off.
- Professional scanning, 16 MP camera, front-facing NFC, and optional UWB. The 2026-09-02 spec and NPA no longer list UHF RFID; the Battlecard still does.
- 302/278 g handheld configurations, a replaceable 4900 mAh standard / 3500 mAh optional battery, IP65, and 1.5 m drop resistance.
- Battlecard compares SP35 with Zebra PS30 and PS20; competitor values remain attributed to that presentation and are not UROVO product specifications.
- New Product Announcement (2026-09-02) covers auto-scan cart mount, tap-to-pay SoftPOS, membership-card unlock, and auto-lock cradle loss prevention. It occupies `others/` because the Battlecard already fills the single `presentation/` slot.
