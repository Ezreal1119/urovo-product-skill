## Important Source-Specific Differences

- **DT50S:** the `DT50` panel states Android 16 and a 16 MP rear camera in body copy, while current dedicated DT50S sources state Android 11/13 and a 13 MP rear camera.
- **SR5750:** body copy says 1200 mAh; the icon says 1150 mAh. The dedicated specification says 1150 mAh.
- **RFG91:** standard battery printed as 5000 mAh (optional 6700 mAh). Dedicated specification and accessory guide state 4900 mAh.
- **UPad:** brochure states a 7 mm profile against the 11 mm specification-table value.
- **P8100P 4G:** brochure icons print IP67, agreeing with the dedicated specification and disagreeing with the presentation's IP65 slide.
- **SP35:** this NA page 5 panel is a personal-shopper marketing block (scanner, NFC, Q-6690, Android 15, Wi-Fi 6E, UWB, 5-inch touch). Prefer the dedicated 2026-09-02 specification for hard specs.
