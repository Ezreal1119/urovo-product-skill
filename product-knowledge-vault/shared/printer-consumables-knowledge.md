## Content Scope

- Thermal (no ribbon; heat-sensitive coating; typical archive ~半年) vs thermal-transfer (ribbon; ~两年到五年 depending on ribbon quality). Scratch test: thermal paper darkens; label stock does not.
- Ribbon classes in body text: **蜡基**, **混合基** (source **村脂**), **树脂**, with speed/concentration hints (e.g. mixed ~100 speed / 8 concentration).
- How to find the coated side of a ribbon (scratch or press with label adhesive).
- Infographic adds **全树脂** and **洗水唛** pairings (copperplate / silver PET / wash-care labels) that the body list does not name.
