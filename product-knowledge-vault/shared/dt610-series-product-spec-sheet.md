## Edition Comparison

| Field | DT610 Std Edition | DT610 Pro Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 165 × 76.2 × 12.9 mm | 169 × 78.2 × 15.8 mm |
| Weight | 222 g | 270 g |
| Display | 6.0-inch 1080×2160 LCD | 6.0-inch 1080×2160 LCD, 600 nits |
| Battery | 4000 mAh / 15.4 Wh; 4950 mAh optional | 5200 mAh / 21.175 Wh |
| Rear camera | 16 MP PDAF | 50 MP PDAF; optional 3D structured-light camera |
| RFID | Built-in, up to 1.5 m, 50 tags/s | Impinj Gen2X, up to 1.5 m, 50+ tags/s |
| Drop | 1.5 m; 1.8 m with rugged boot | 1.8 m |
| Sealing | IP67 | IP68 |

Both editions list Qualcomm Q-6690 up to 2.9 GHz, Android 15 upgradable to Android 19, 8 GB / 128 GB UFS with 12 GB / 256 GB optional, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, 5G, hot swap, fingerprint authentication, and standard/FlexRange scan-engine options.

## Active Source Discrepancy

The shared series sheet states a 5200 mAh DT610 Pro standard battery. The product-specific DT610 Pro specification, current accessories guide, and 2026-06-25 presentation state 5500 mAh. Treat the capacity as unresolved and surface the warning from [[products/DT610-Pro|DT610 Pro Source Hub]].
