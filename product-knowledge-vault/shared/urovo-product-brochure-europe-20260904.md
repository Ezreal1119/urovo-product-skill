## Important Source-Specific Differences

- **RT30 sealing:** this brochure's page 5 panel still prints **IP67**. The current dedicated RT30 specification and product brief print **IP65**. Recorded on the RT30 Hub.
- **SP35:** the page 8 first bullet no longer lists RFID; UWB is optional. That RFID drop agrees with the current 2026-09-02 dedicated specification and NPA. The Battlecard still lists optional RFID.
- **DT50S:** this brochure's `DT50` panel states Android 16 and a 16 MP camera in body copy, while current dedicated DT50S sources state Android 11/13 and a 13 MP rear camera; the same brochure panel's camera icon also says 13 MP.
- **DT610 Pro:** brochure and shared 2026 series sheet state a 5200 mAh battery while the dedicated spec states 5500 mAh. Brochure/shared series material says Bluetooth 6.0, while the dedicated spec's wireless-PAN table says Bluetooth 5.0 even though its own marketing narrative also says 6.0.
- **SR5750:** the brochure page itself says 1200 mAh in body copy and 1150 mAh in its icon; the dedicated specification says 1150 mAh.
- **RFG91:** brochure and device/accessory guide state a 5000 mAh standard battery; dedicated specification and accessory guide state 4900 mAh. Optional 6700 mAh agrees.
- **U2S:** brochure states 3500 mAh, while dedicated specification/brochure state 3360 mAh.
- **UPad:** brochure states a 7 mm profile, supporting the existing “under 7 mm” marketing side of the unresolved difference against the 11 mm specification-table value.
