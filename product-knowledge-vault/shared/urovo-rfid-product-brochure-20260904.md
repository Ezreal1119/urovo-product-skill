## Facts Used by Vault Pages

- DT610 is positioned as the first enterprise mobile processor integrating UHF RFID (Qualcomm Dragonwing Q-6690).
- DT630 optional RFID: Impinj Gen2X, over 50 tags/sec, 1.2 m range in this brochure.
- DT610 Pro optional all-in-one RFID reading and barcode scanning; battery callout 5200 mAh (wide-temperature), hot-swap.
- DT610 Std: RFID reading and barcode scanning; 4000 mAh smart battery with hot-swap; in-display NFC.
- DT50D: Impinj E310; 1.5 m / 3 m UHF RFID icons; Android 11 with Android 13 optional.
- UPad: in-display RFID reader; 7 mm profile; 8000 mAh.
- DT50P: Impinj E710; 20 m+; IP67.
- DT50P Lite: Impinj E710 / Gen2X; 1300+ tags/sec; over 15 m; 498 g; 6500 mAh.
- U100: optional short-range UHF RFID; face recognition and NFC membership.
- RFG91: 1300+ tags/sec; >15 m with DT610; 5000 mAh standard / 6700 mAh optional; IP67.
- FR7000 Series, FR2000, FR1000, D81R Series, and K388 Pro (optional RFID encoding) occupy the fixed-reader and RFID-printer panels.

## Important Source-Specific Differences

- **RFG91 standard battery:** this brochure prints 5000 mAh; dedicated specification and accessory guide state 4900 mAh.
- **UPad thickness:** 7 mm marketing profile versus the 11 mm specification-table value.
- **DT610 Pro battery:** 5200 mAh here versus 5500 mAh on the dedicated Pro specification.
- **DT630 RFID range:** 1.2 m in this brochure. Prefer the dedicated DT630 sources for current hard-spec answers and surface any Hub discrepancy already recorded there.
