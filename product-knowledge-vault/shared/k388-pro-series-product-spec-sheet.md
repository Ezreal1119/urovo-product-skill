## Content Scope

- K388 Pro 2’’: 2-inch compact labeling terminal, up to 48 mm print width and 120 mm/s; SM6225; Android 14, upgradable to Android 20; 800 g.
- K388 Pro 4’’: 4-inch wide-format labeling terminal, up to 104 mm print width and 100 mm/s; 992 g. Series page 5 drop column is 1.2 m; the dedicated 4-inch spec says 1.5 m — see the Hub discrepancy table.
- K388 Pro BT: 2-inch Bluetooth labeling terminal with a host-free design; ESP32-D0WDR2; 700 g; Bluetooth v4.2.
- Series positioning, workflows, three printing modes, optional UHF RFID tag encoding (series page 6 spans 2’’ and 4’’ columns), Android/UEE software, connectivity, durability, and accessories.
