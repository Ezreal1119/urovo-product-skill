## Content Scope

- K388 Pro: 2-inch compact labeling terminal, up to 48 mm print width and 120 mm/s.
- K388 Pro 4: 4-inch wide-format labeling terminal, up to 108 mm print width and 90 mm/s.
- K388 Pro BT: 2-inch Bluetooth labeling terminal with a host-free design.
- Series positioning, workflows, three printing modes, optional UHF RFID tag encoding, Android/UEE software, connectivity, durability, and accessories.
