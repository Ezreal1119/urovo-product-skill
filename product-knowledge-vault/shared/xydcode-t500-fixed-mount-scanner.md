## Key Facts

- 1280 × 1080 image sensor with red-LED illumination.
- Reads the listed 1D and 2D symbologies, including QR Code, Micro QR, Data Matrix, and PDF417.
- Resolution ≥3 mil; typical Code 128 5 mil depth of field 45-90 mm; EAN-13 13 mil 0-130 mm.
- 42.5° horizontal × 36.5° vertical field of view; roll 360°, pitch/skew ±50°.
- USB and RS232 interfaces; 5.0 V ±5%; <450 mA operating and <180 mA standby.
- 155 × 155.5 × 93.8 mm; 480 g.
- -20°C to 50°C operation; -40°C to 70°C storage; 5%-95% non-condensing; up to 100,000 lux.
- The companion includes every dimension visible in the page-3 engineering drawing.
