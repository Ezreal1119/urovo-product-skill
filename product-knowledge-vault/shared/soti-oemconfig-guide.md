## Covered Product Hubs

This source is **platform-wide**: it documents a generic SOTI + Urovo OEMConfig deployment flow that applies to the UROVO Android portfolio, not to a specific product subset. It therefore links no individual Product Hub. For the OEMConfig configuration reference, see [[shared/urovo-oemconfig-manual|Urovo OEMConfig User Manual]]; for MDM validation status by product, see [[shared/mdm-certification-summary|MDM Certification Summary]].

## Content Scope

- Step 1: factory reset the device, scan the SOTI registration code, and connect to an external WiFi network.
- Step 2: after SOTI registration, wait for refresh/sync and confirm registration in the web console; find the latest OEMConfig version (1.3.42 at the time of writing) and click "Edit".
- Step 3: configure, save, push (下发), and select the corresponding device.
- Step 4: verify on the device whether OEMConfig has been downloaded.
