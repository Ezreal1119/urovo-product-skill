## Page 1 — Cover, Meet the DT610 Pro

**Certifications shown on cover:** Google Mobile Service, Android Enterprise Recommended, ARCore, Qualcomm, RAIN Alliance.

**Tagline:** *The Next Generation of Frontline Power*

### Meet the DT610 Pro

DT610 Pro combines cutting-edge technology with enterprise-grade durability, purpose-built for mission-critical frontline operations. It integrates edge AI, next-generation connectivity, and rugged design to enable faster, smarter, and more efficient workflows.

Powered by the Qualcomm® Q-6690, its octa-core CPU is upgradeable up to 2.9 GHz, delivering high-performance edge AI for real-time tasks like OCR, speech, and computer vision — while ensuring future-ready scalability to protect your long-term investment.

With 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and 2×2 MU-MIMO, the DT610 Pro achieves up to 4.8× faster speeds, 4× lower latency, and 5× greater capacity compared to Wi-Fi 6. With Bluetooth 6.0 for short-range connectivity and CBRS support for private cellular networks, it ensures seamless, low-latency, multi-network connectivity for always-on operations.

Engineered for real-world use, it features a lightweight yet rugged design with IP68 protection, a high-brightness display, hot-swappable batteries, and advanced data capture capabilities, delivering uninterrupted productivity across every shift across industries such as logistics, warehousing, field service, and retail.

DT610 Pro — the next evolution of frontline productivity.

## Page 2 — Product Highlights (Part 1)

### 1. Slim Design, Superior Protection

Featuring enhanced raised-edge protection (Higher Wall Protection) in a sleek form factor. At just 270 g, it delivers effortless portability without compromising durability.

### 2. Purpose-Built for Extreme Conditions

With an IP68 rating for full dust and water protection, the device performs reliably in heavy rain and harsh industrial environments. Built for real-world durability, it withstands drops of up to 1.8 m across the full operating temperature range without a case, and is validated through 1,000 consecutive 1.0 m tumbles for exceptional shock resilience.

### 3. Breakthrough Enterprise Processor

Powered by the world's first enterprise mobile processor with fully integrated UHF (RAIN) RFID capabilities, the DT610 Pro features an octa-core CPU up to 2.9 GHz, 6 INT8 TOPS of on-device AI performance, and comes with Android 15 out of the box — delivering exceptional responsiveness for demanding workflows.

### 4. Next-Gen Wireless

Powered by 5G and Wi-Fi 7 (2+2 MU-MIMO) with 6 GHz support, the DT610 Pro delivers up to 4.8× faster speeds and expanded bandwidth for superior throughput. By comparison with Wi-Fi 6, it achieves 4× lower latency and 5× greater capacity, ensuring ultra-responsive performance in high-density environments.

Combined with Bluetooth 6.0, it enables low-latency, multi-device connectivity with optimized power efficiency — keeping mission-critical operations running in real time.

### 5. Brilliant FHD+ Display

The 6-inch FHD+ screen (1080 × 2160) delivers sharp details, vibrant colors, and wide viewing angles. A 600-nit high-brightness panel ensures clear visibility even in bright sunlight, such as in a truck cab, with excellent readability in both portrait and landscape orientations.

### 6. Advanced Scan Camera with 3D Sensing Capabilities

DT610 Pro features a 50 MP rear autofocus camera (PDAF) that captures clear, detailed images even in low light — ideal for visual documentation in logistics workflows. It enables accurate recording of package conditions upon arrival, including damage, moisture exposure, and deformation.

With ARCore support, it enables fast autofocus and multi-barcode scanning, while optional 3D sensing allows accurate volume measurement for inventory and shipping, enhancing warehouse efficiency and accuracy.

### 7. Choose Standard or Advanced Scanning

The FlexRange scan engine has a flexible working range — from 5 cm to 10 m. Its impressive reading range and speed enable users to read barcodes without having to bend, stretch, or climb ladders, making scanning safer and more efficient.

The standard 1D/2D scan engine delivers fast, accurate barcode capture with stable performance in all lighting conditions, including bright sunlight.

### 8. AI-Powered Smart Battery Management Featuring Hot-Swap Flexibility

DT610 Pro features a 5500 mAh battery, delivering reliable full-shift endurance. AI-powered battery management dynamically optimizes efficiency.

Hot-swap support ensures uninterrupted operation, while its wide-temperature design guarantees stable performance in extreme conditions.

### 9. Dual-sided NFC

Supports dual-sided NFC reading, enabling fast and flexible interactions from any angle — ideal for logistics and warehouse operations, including quick payments, ID verification, and card emulation, while improving workflow efficiency and reducing handling time.

## Page 3 — Product Highlights (Part 2)

### 10. Biometric Security

A fingerprint sensor integrated into the power button enables instant, secure user authentication with a single touch.

### 11. Loud & Clear with Noise Cancellation

DT610 Pro is equipped with powerful front-facing stereo speakers delivering over 100 dB loud output, ensuring clear communication even in noisy industrial environments. Combined with advanced noise reduction technology, it enables crystal-clear push-to-talk interactions for seamless coordination across teams and scenarios.

### 12. Integrated RFID

Equipped with Impinj Gen2X RFID, the DT610 Pro reads 50+ tags per second at distances up to 1.5 meters, enabling fast and accurate inventory and asset tracking. Ideal for warehouse management, retail stocktaking, and asset tracking, it streamlines operations with greater efficiency and reliability.

### 13. Visualized Fleet Management

UROVO UMS (Unified Management System) provides visualized management, enabling IT administrators to centrally manage multiple mobile devices from a single dashboard across different device types and operating systems. It supports shared device access control, application whitelisting and blacklisting, as well as granular user and device group permissions. From deployment to decommissioning, UMS streamlines device and smart battery lifecycle management, remote data wipe, and password policy enforcement — helping enterprises protect critical data while improving workforce productivity.

### 14. Streamlined Device Setup & Enterprise Management

UROVO U-Stage is designed to make device setup faster and simpler, helping customers get all their devices ready for use with minimal effort. By creating a staging file with the required configurations, settings, and applications, devices can be automatically set up through remote deployment, local file import, or QR code scanning — without manual procedures.

OEMConfig also integrates with enterprise EMM platforms, enabling customized policy deployment, permission control, and compliance management across devices.
