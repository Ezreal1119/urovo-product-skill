# DT610 Pro Ultra-Rugged Mobile Computer

Integrated Barcode Scanning and RFID Reading

Powerful Performance

Powered by the Qualcomm octa-core up to 2.9 GHz CPU and enhancedwith Al capabilities, the device handles complex workflows with ease anddelivers fast, stable performance across enterprise applications
Integrated FlexRange scanning and RFID technology enable fast, accurate data capture from 5 cm to 10 m, streamlining inventory management, stocktaking, and asset tracking with greater efficiency, safety, and reliability.

Clear Visibility for Indoor and Outdoor Logistics
Enhanced Durability

Featuring IP68 protection,1.8m drop resistance, and explosion-proofcapabilities,the device provides exceptional defense against dust, water,and impact,ensuring reliable performance in harsh environments.
The 6-inch 1080x2160 HD display with 600 nits brightness and anti-glaredesign ensures clear readability indoors and outdoors,supporting efficientlogistics operations.

Advanced Camera with 3D Structured Light Camera
Next-Gen Wireless

With 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and 2×2 MU-MIMO, the DT610 Pro achieves up to 4.8× faster speeds, 4× lower latency, and 5× greater capacity compared to Wi-Fi 6.
The 50MP PDAF camera enables clear package documentation, while ARCore-powered scanning and optional 3D volume measurement improve warehouse efficiency and accuracy.
