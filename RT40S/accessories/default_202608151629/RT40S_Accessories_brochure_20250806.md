# RT40S Accessories Brochure

> **Canonical original:** `RT40S/accessories/default_202608151629/RT40S_Accessories_brochure_20250806.pdf`
> **Document date:** 2025-08-06 (from the source filename)
> **Language:** English
> **Scope:** Accessories for the RT40S Industrial Mobile Computer.

## Accessories Overview

- **Adaptor**, **USB Cable**, **Battery**
- **Hand Strap – 1** (standard version RT40S), **Hand Strap – 2** (RT40S with gun grip), **Holster**
- **Charging Cradle**, **4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle**, **Gun Grip**, **5-Slot Charging Cradle (5 devices)**, **5-Slot Charging Cradle (4 devices, 4 batteries)**, **Boot Case**

## Accessories List

### Adaptor / USB Cable / Battery

- **Adaptor:** QC3.0 quick charge; input 100–240 V ~ 50/60 Hz 0.6 A MAX; output 5 V/3 A, 9 V/2 A, 12 V/1.5 A
- **USB Cable:** supports charging and data transferring; port: Type-C
- **Battery:** removable 3.85 V, 5200 mAh
  - BTY-RT40-52-02 (only for overseas export)
  - BTY-RT40-52-03 (only for overseas export)
  - BTY-RT40S-52-01, BTY-RT40S-52-02, BTY-RT40S-52-03

### Hand Strap – 1 / Hand Strap – 2

- **Hand Strap – 1:** supports standard version RT40S; convenient for mobile operation and carrying; HS-RT40P-01
- **Hand Strap – 2:** supports RT40S with gun grip; convenient for mobile operation and carrying

### Holster

- Supports standard version RT40S and RT40S with gun grip
- Independent battery bag for extended battery life
- Standard with shoulder strap, optional support belt
- SH-RT40-01, SH-RT40-02

### Charging Cradle

- Supports charging one device and one battery
- Supports standard version RT40S, gun-grip version RT40S, and RT40S with boot case
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging, Type-C for data
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Standard adapter: CRD-RT40-05; American standard: CRD-RT40-09; European standard: CRD-RT40-10; British standard: CRD-RT40-18

### 4-Slot Battery Charging Cradle

- Supports charging 4 batteries
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/10 A
- Standard adapter: CRD-RT40-03; European standard: CRD-RT40-14; American standard: CRD-RT40-15; British standard: CRD-RT40-16

### Gun Grip

- Ergonomic design
- Efficient scanning with trigger button
- UROVO English packaging: TRG-RT40-03; Chinese packaging: TRG-RT40-04

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle (5 devices)

- Supports charging 5 devices
- Supports standard version RT40S, gun-grip version RT40S, and RT40S with boot case
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/5 A
- Standard adapter: CRD-RT40-04; British standard: CRD-RT40-11; European standard: CRD-RT40-13; American standard: CRD-RT40-17

### 5-Slot Charging Cradle (4 devices, 4 batteries)

- Supports charging 4 devices and 4 batteries
- Supports standard version RT40S, gun-grip version RT40S, and RT40S with boot case
- LED charging status indicator
- Port: DC jack for charging
- Charging cradle adapter output: 12 V/10 A
- Standard adapter: CRD-RT40-01; American standard: CRD-RT40-06; British standard: CRD-RT40-07; European standard: CRD-RT40-08

### Boot Case

- Supports standard version RT40S and gun-grip version RT40S
- BC-RT40-01
