### Meet the UPad — Your Frontline Hub for Smarter Retail

UPad is a next-generation commercial mobility platform designed for modern retail and enterprise operations. Combining mobility, payment, data capture, and modular scalability in a sleek and portable form factor, it helps businesses streamline workflows and improve operational efficiency.

Integrated barcode scanning, RFID reading, SoftPOS payment, and Wi-Fi 6E connectivity enable seamless transactions, real-time data synchronization, and flexible mobile operations. More than just a tablet, UPad is built for the future of intelligent and connected business operations.

## Page 2 — Operational Flexibility & Productivity, Enterprise-Grade Performance & Reliability (Part 1)

### Operational Flexibility & Productivity

**Innovative Quick-Lock Modular Design**

The magnetic feature and snap-on design on device back enables secure and easy attachment to and removal from modular accessories to cater to different operation scenarios in seconds.

**Incredibly Light. Exceptionally Slim.**

Combining a lightweight 500 g design with an ultra-slim profile under 7 mm, the device redefines portable productivity for commercial users. The large 11-inch WUXGA display offers a wider, clearer viewing experience for retail, service, and mobile operations, all within a sleek precision-crafted metal body built for everyday business use.

**Dual-Mode Data Capture**

Integrated with high performance barcode scanner and in-display RFID reader enables easy and fast reading of barcodes and RFID tags.

**All-in-one SoftPOS Payment**

Turn your display into a payment terminal. Customers can tap to pay via on-screen NFC, with transaction amounts clearly shown on the large display. The system is EMVCo PCD L2 certified for security. It also accelerates checkout by supporting QR code scanning and member card verification.

**Multi-scene Accessory Pairings**

Upad accessories optimize multi-scenario operations — enabling simplified checkouts, and seamless check-ins for retail, hotels, and restaurants alike. From tableside ordering to kitchen workflows, our ecosystem enhances both staff efficiency and guest satisfaction.

### Enterprise-Grade Performance & Reliability (Part 1)

**Reconstructing Performance Experience for Commercial Scenarios**

Powered by a latest Qualcomm octa-core CPU with 2.2 GHz on a 4 nm process, paired with memory options of 4 GB + 64 GB or 8 GB + 128 GB, ensures retailers growing operation needs.

**Seamless, High-Performance Enterprise-Grade Connectivity**

Equipped with dual antennas and supporting tri-band (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz), this device ensures seamless and robust wireless coverage across the entire commercial premises — from the storefront to the back office.

**AI-Powered Smart Battery Management**

Power through your day with the UPad. Featuring a service-replaceable, 8,000 mAh battery and AI smart-charging technology, it delivers sustained performance and extended battery lifespan — ensuring reliable power through the toughest shifts.
