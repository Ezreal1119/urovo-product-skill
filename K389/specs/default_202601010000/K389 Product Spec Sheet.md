## Page 1 — Cover, Meet the K389

**Tagline:** *Engineered for Endurance, Designed for Mobility.*

### Meet the K389

Built for demanding industrial and mobile environments, the K389 3-inch portable printer features an exclusive rugged casing that withstands 2-meter drops onto concrete, ensuring reliable performance in warehouse and manufacturing operations.

Designed for enterprise-grade workflows, it delivers consistent printing even under intensive daily use across logistics, field service, and industrial applications. Equipped with IEEE 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac (Wi-Fi 5) dual-band connectivity (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz), it enables fast, stable, and low-latency wireless communication for seamless system integration and uninterrupted printing.

A high-capacity 3200 mAh battery provides up to double the runtime of previous models, supporting extended shifts with ease.

With robust durability, long-lasting power, and advanced wireless performance, the K389 ensures continuous connectivity and maximized productivity where it matters most.

## Page 2 — Product Highlights

### 1. Engineered for Endurance: The Rugged Mobile Printer

The K389 sets a new standard for resilience, purpose-built for the toughest manufacturing and warehouse environments. Featuring an exclusive protective casing, it withstands 2-meter drops onto concrete from any angle, ensuring reliable operation through the most demanding shifts.

### 2. Compact Design for On-the-Spot Printing

Maximize efficiency with true mobility. The K389, weighing only 390 g, is engineered for portability. Carry it effortlessly throughout your shift to instantly print labels where and when you need them — eliminating unnecessary movement between picking locations and printers, and accelerating workflows in fast-paced warehouse environments.

### 3. Double the Shift, Single Charge

Maximize productivity with uninterrupted power. The K389 houses a high-capacity 3200 mAh battery paired with intelligent power management, delivering up to double the runtime of previous models. It powers the longest workday without fail.

### 4. Seamless Connectivity for a Mobile Workforce

Stay connected and in sync. The K389 delivers robust, reliable wireless connectivity tailored for industrial environments, supporting 802.11 ac dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz), WPA/WPA2 security — ensuring a stable, secure connection as your team moves seamlessly across the facility.

### 5. High-Speed Mobile Printing with Precision

Delivering fast and reliable output for high-demand mobile printing scenarios, the device achieves a maximum print speed of up to 100 mm/s enabling efficient processing of labels and receipts in time-critical operations. Combined with 203 dpi (8 dots/mm) high-resolution printing, it ensures sharp, legible barcodes, text, and graphics even at high throughput.
