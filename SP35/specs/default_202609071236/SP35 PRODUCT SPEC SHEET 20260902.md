## Page 1 — Positioning

**Meet the SP35 — Intelligence for Shopping, Efficiency for Retail**

SP35 is a self-shopping device for the full journey from product search through scanning and checkout. Integrated with optional UWB technology, professional barcode scanning, camera and NFC, it supports precise positioning, fast product recognition, automatic cart updates, and contactless payment. Powered by Qualcomm® Q-6690 processor and Android 15, it delivers powerful performance and is ready for future retail applications.

RFID is **not** named on this page.
