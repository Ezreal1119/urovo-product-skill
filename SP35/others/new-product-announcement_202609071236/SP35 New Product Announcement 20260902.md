## Slide 7 — Faster Scanning, Clearer Capture

- Integrated professional-grade scanner supports 1D/2D barcodes, including damaged, scratched, or stained labels.
- Rear 16 MP high-resolution camera for visual-information capture.
- Visual callouts show the top scan window with red illumination and the rear circular camera module.

## Slide 8 — Shop Effortlessly With Auto-scan Mode

Auto-scan mode supports hands-free scanning. The visual shows SP35 mounted vertically in a white shopping-cart holder while a shopper presents a packaged item to the scan window.

## Slide 9 — Seamless Self-checkout with Tap to Pay

Built-in NFC and SoftPOS enable tap-to-pay without extra hardware. The two visuals show a smartphone and a Visa contactless card tapped near the top of the SP35 screen.

## Slide 10 — Smart Device Management for Loss Prevention

- Tap a membership card to unlock SP35, either on the docked device or on a nearby kiosk/terminal.
- Auto-lock cradle helps prevent device loss.
- Visuals show multi-bay cradle banks, lock/checkmark screen states, and a mechanical lock inset.
