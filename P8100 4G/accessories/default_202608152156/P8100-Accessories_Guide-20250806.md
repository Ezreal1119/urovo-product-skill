## Slide 4 — Accessories List-1 (Standard)

### Adaptor
- Input: 100–240 V~ 50/60 Hz, 0.6 A MAX
- Output: QC3.0 adaptor (5 V / 3 A, 9 V / 2 A, 12 V / 1.5 A)

### USB Cable
- Support Charging and Data Transferring
- Port: USB Type-C

### Battery
- Removable 3.85 V 8400 mAh
- Model: **BTY-P8100-84-03**

*Visual:* Photos of the black wall charger, coiled Type-C cable, and battery label.
