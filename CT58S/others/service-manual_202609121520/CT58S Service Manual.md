## 1. Disassembly steps

### 1）、Rear Cover Removal (page 3)

首选取掉绿色圈内的**3 颗带防水圈的六角螺丝**，在取掉红色圈内**8 颗普通六角螺丝**, and remove the card tray.

Gently pry along the gap between the rear case assembly and the front case bracket to remove the rear case.

**Visual:** rear of a CT58S-class phablet. Green circles on **3** hex screws with sealing washers; red circles on **8** ordinary hex screws (11 fasteners total plus the card tray). Rear shell then separated from the front-shell / display assembly.

### 2）、 Rear camera removal (page 4)

首先取掉摄像头FPC 排扣，在取掉HALL FPC 排扣，取下支架.

**Visual:** opened chassis; camera FPC latch and HALL FPC latch released; metal camera bracket lifted; rear-camera module beside the board. Board has a large copper / shield can.

### 3）、Motherboard removal (page 5)

取掉屏的排扣、主FPC 排扣、扫描头排扣和同轴线座；拆下主板螺丝**3 个**和固定扫描头螺丝**2 个**；拆下主板.

**Visual:** mainboard still in the chassis with FPC/coaxial seats; then the board removed. Scan-engine still on the front assembly in one photo.

### 4）、Scan engine removal (page 6)

**HS7：** 拆掉固定扫描头上的**2 颗螺丝**，从扫描头支架中取出扫描头，拆掉排线.

**2030S：** 拆掉固定扫描头上的**2 颗螺丝**，从扫描头支架中取出扫描头，拆掉排线.

**Visual:** two scan-engine photos (HS7 and 2030S layouts). Each shows the engine in a bracket with FPC; two screws called out.

### 5）、USB board removal (page 7)

First unplug the coaxial cable on the USB board, put the coaxial cable aside, and remove the **2 screws** on the USB board.

Remove the motherboard-USB board cable buckle, carefully lift up the USB board and remove it.

**Visual:** USB / I/O board at the bottom of the chassis; coaxial unplugged; two screws; board lifted.

### 6）、Precautions when replacing the display (page 8)

主板下方的听筒，光感小板，硅胶垫，导光硅胶需要拆除.

**Visual:** display assembly in the front shell; earpiece, light-sense sub-board, silicone pad, and light-guide silicone called out under the motherboard area.
