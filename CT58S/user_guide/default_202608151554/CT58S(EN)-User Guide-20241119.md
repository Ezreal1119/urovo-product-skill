## Getting Started

### Major Parts

(Device illustrated with front/rear parts; layout shown in the original document.)

### Install / Replace Battery

1. Slide the rear cover lock to the right.
2. Remove the battery cover as shown in the figure.
3. Install/replace the battery.
4. Place the back cover fully, then push the lock buckle to the left.

### Installing SD / SIM Card

1. Remove the battery cover and battery.
2. Pull out the SIM card slot. Place the Nano-SIM or Micro SD/TF card into the respective slot, then push the card slot in completely.
3. Install the battery. Place the back cover fully, then push the lock buckle to the right.

Note: The SIM card is a Nano-SIM card.

Attention: Use proper electrostatic discharge (ESD) precautions to avoid damaging the SIM card. Proper ESD precautions include, but are not limited to, working on an ESD mat and ensuring the user is properly grounded.

### Charging the Device

To charge the main battery:

1. Connect the charging accessory to the appropriate power source.
2. Insert the CT58S into a cradle or attach to a cable. The CT58S turns on and begins charging. The Charging/Notification LED is red, then turns green when fully charged.

Charging methods: charging with cradle, or charging with USB cable.

### Charging Precautions

1. Do not use or charge the device in any environment with too high or low temperature.
2. Charge the battery only with the product's standard-configuration charger or desktop charger.
3. If the device (including the battery) is not used for the moment, store the battery and device separately and pack them in PE bags or other insulated bags in the original package to prevent dampness or contact with other electronic devices. Do not store unpacked batteries in a stack.
4. Do not idle the device for over 3 months; charge and discharge an unused battery once per 3 months. The stored battery should be 60%–70% charged (i.e., 3.8 V–3.9 V).
5. The lithium battery should be stored somewhere cool, dry, and ventilated. Storage in a 0–28 °C environment is advised.

**Charging indicators:**

| State | LED | Indication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | — | CT58S is not charging; not inserted correctly in the cradle or connected to a power source; the charger/cradle is not powered |
| Long red light | Red | CT58S is charging |
| Long green light | Green | Charging complete |

### Turn On/Off CT58S

**Turn on:**

1. In the shutdown state, long press the "power key".
2. When the CT58S goes into startup mode, it vibrates.
3. Release the "power key" and wait until the CT58S finishes booting.

If you cannot turn on the CT58S, try the steps below:

1. The battery needs to be charged to a certain level; charge first.
2. Press and hold the "power key" for over 10 seconds to force reboot.

**Turn off:** Press the power button for a long time in the power-on state until the shutdown prompt appears. Click "Power off". Touch to restart the device.

## Using the Device

### Unlocking the Screen

Setting method: open "Settings–Security–Screen lock".

Use the lock screen to protect access to data on the device. Some email accounts require locking the screen. When locked, a pattern, PIN, or password is required to unlock the device. Press the Power button to lock the screen; the device also locks after a pre-defined timeout. Press and release the Power button to wake the device; the lock screen displays. Swipe the screen up to unlock.

- **Pattern screen:** unlock with pattern. If the pattern screen unlock feature is enabled, the Pattern screen appears instead of the Lock screen.
- **PIN screen / Password screen:** if the PIN or password screen unlock feature is enabled, enter the PIN or password after unlocking the screen. If the user enters the PIN, password, or pattern incorrectly five times, they must wait 30 seconds before trying again. If the user forgets the PIN, password, or pattern, contact the system administrator.

## Maintenance and Troubleshooting

### Maintenance Equipment

For trouble-free service, observe the following tips when using the CT58S:

- Do not scratch the screen of the CT58S.
- The touch-sensitive screen is glass. Do not drop the CT58S or subject it to strong impact.
- Protect the CT58S from temperature extremes. Do not leave it on a car dashboard on a hot day, and keep it away from heat sources.
- Do not store or use the CT58S in any location that is dusty, damp, or wet.
- Use a soft lens cloth to clean the CT58S. If the screen surface becomes soiled, clean it with a soft cloth moistened with diluted window-cleaning solution.

**Long-term storage:** when storing the device for long periods, ensure the battery has at least 50% charge and store at -40–70 °C.

**Cleaning instructions:** do not apply liquid directly to the device; dampen a soft cloth or use pre-moistened wipes. Do not wrap the device in the cloth or wipe; gently wipe the unit. Avoid letting liquid pool around the display window or other places. Allow the unit to air dry before use.

### Cleaning Equipment

**Cleaning materials required:** alcohol wipes, lens tissue, cotton-tipped applicators, isopropyl alcohol, can of compressed air with a tube.

**Cleaning frequency:** because mobile devices are used in different environments, cleaning frequency is determined by the user. If used in a dirty environment, clean the camera window regularly for optimal performance.

**Cleaning the CT58S:**

- **Housing:** using alcohol wipes, wipe the housing including the buttons.
- **Display:** wipe down with alcohol wipes, but avoid letting liquid pool around the edges. Immediately dry with a soft, non-abrasive cloth to prevent streaking.
- **Camera and exit window:** wipe periodically with lens tissue or other material suitable for cleaning optical material.

**Connector cleaning:**

1. Dip the cotton portion of the cotton-tipped applicator in isopropyl alcohol.
2. Rub the cotton portion back and forth across the connector. Do not leave cotton residue.
3. Repeat at least three times.
4. Use the applicator dipped in alcohol to remove grease and dirt near the connector area.
5. Use a dry cotton-tipped applicator and repeat.
6. Spray compressed air on the connector area, pointing the tube/nozzle about ½ inch away from the surface.
7. Inspect for grease or dirt and repeat if required.

**Cleaning cradle connectors:**

1. Remove the DC power cable from the cradle.
2. Dip the cotton portion of the applicator in isopropyl alcohol.
3. Rub along the pins of the connector, moving slowly back and forth from one side to the other. Do not leave cotton residue.
4. Rub all sides of the connector.
5. Spray compressed air, pointing the tube/nozzle about ½ inch from the surface.
6. Remove any lint left by the applicator.
7. If grease or dirt is found on other areas of the cradle, use a lint-free cloth and alcohol to remove them.
8. Allow at least 10 to 30 minutes (depending on ambient temperature and humidity) for the alcohol to air dry before applying power to the cradle. Low temperature and high humidity require longer drying time.
