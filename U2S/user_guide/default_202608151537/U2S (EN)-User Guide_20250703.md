## About the Battery

The U2S uses a rechargeable lithium-ion battery. Battery life depends on factors including display brightness, display timeout, input devices, temperature, and usage conditions.

### Battery Identification

| --- | --- |

Use the original factory-supplied battery. Contact the local sales representative for a replacement.

### Battery Safety

> Use only the manufacturer-specified charger. Do not disassemble, impact, crush, or burn the battery. Stop using a severely swollen battery. Do not expose it to high temperatures or use it after water immersion.

- Do not store the battery above 60°C (140°F), including in a sun-heated vehicle or near a radiator or heat source.
- Immediately replace and correctly dispose of a battery that is deformed, swollen, discolored, or too hot to handle.
- Do not disassemble, open, crush, bend, deform, puncture, or shred it.
- Do not drop it or subject it to mechanical shock or pressure.
- Do not short-circuit it or allow metal or conductive objects to touch the terminals.
- Do not expose it to fire, microwaves, dryers, or other high-temperature environments.
- Do not modify or refurbish it, insert foreign objects, submerge it in water or another liquid, or expose it to fire, explosion, or other hazards.
- Keep the charging area free of debris, flammable material, and chemicals. Take extra care in non-commercial charging environments.
- Store batteries out of children's reach.
- Follow local rules for disposal. Cover discarded battery terminals with electrical insulating tape.
- Seek immediate medical attention if a battery is swallowed.
- If a battery leaks, avoid the liquid. After contact, rinse with plenty of water and seek medical advice.

### Battery Use and Storage

- Reduce consumption with a short screen timeout, adaptive brightness, disabled unnecessary background apps/services, Battery Saver, Battery Management, and Airplane Mode when connectivity is unnecessary.
- Lithium-ion capacity declines with repeated cycles and extreme temperature or humidity. An aging battery may discharge faster and charge more slowly.
- Charge between 0°C and 40°C (32°F to 104°F).
- Use only compatible manufacturer-specified batteries and chargers.
- Replace the battery about every two years, or when health falls below 70%.
- Charge or replace the battery promptly at the low-battery indication. Avoid complete discharge.
- Disconnect power after a full charge to avoid overcharging.
- For several days of non-use, install a fully charged battery and enable Airplane Mode or power off the device.
- For storage, charge to about 50% and keep at about 25°C (77°F).
- For longer storage, remove the charged battery or charge it with the battery charger, store device and battery separately in a cool, dry place, and periodically check the battery because it self-discharges.

## Charge Before Use

The U2S has a removable battery. Charge it for at least three hours before first use with the supplied adapter and cable. Using the device while charging increases the charge time. The battery is not installed in a newly unpacked unit.

1. Connect the charging accessory to a suitable power source.
2. Insert the U2S into the magnetic pogo-pin connector. The device turns on and begins charging.

The charging/notification LED is at the upper left of the device front.

| LED state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Not charging. Verify U2S alignment with the pogo-pin connector and that the charger is connected to a powered source. |
| Steady red | Charging. |
| Steady green | Charging complete; remove the power supply promptly. |

## Change the Battery

> Keep hands and all parts dry before installing a battery. Mating wet components can cause damage not covered by warranty.

### Hot Swap

The internal battery temporarily powers the display, Wi-Fi, and running applications while the main battery is removed. Install a fully charged main battery within **60 seconds**; the device should remain on and retain its working state.

- The internal battery is not user-replaceable.
- Do not install or remove a Micro SD card during a hot swap.
- The main battery charges the internal battery. Frequent consecutive hot swaps can deplete it.
- Multiple running applications and extreme temperatures can increase internal-battery drain.
- If the internal battery is depleted, hot swap shuts the device down and unsaved data may be lost. Save important files first. If shutdown occurs, install a new battery and press Power to restart.

### Install the Battery

1. Insert the battery's left side first, slide it left until it stops, and press in the right side.
2. The battery buckle moves right and then returns left to complete installation.

### Replace the Battery

Spare batteries are sold separately.

1. Power off the device or start a hot swap.
2. Slide the battery buckle right to unlock it, then remove the battery.
3. Install the replacement using the preceding installation procedure.

# Chapter 2 - Using the Device

## Install a Micro SD Card

1. Slide the rear-cover lock to **Unlock**.
2. Insert the TF (Micro SD) card in the illustrated slot.
3. Reinstall the rear cover.
4. Slide the cover lock to **Lock**.

## Turn the U2S On, Off, or Restart

### Turn On

1. With the device shut down, press and hold Power.
2. The U2S vibrates as startup begins.
3. Release Power and wait for boot to complete.

If the device does not start, charge it first; if necessary, hold Power for more than 10 seconds to force a reboot.

### Turn Off or Restart

Hold Power while the device is on until the power menu appears, then select **Power off** or **Restart**.

## Unlock and Secure the Screen

The lock screen protects device data. Some email accounts require it. Power immediately locks the screen; it also locks after the configured timeout.

Configure via **Settings > Security and privacy > Screen lock**, then choose:

- None.
- Swipe.
- Pattern.
- PIN.
- Password.

Follow the prompts and exit Settings. Press Power to wake the device, swipe up, and complete the configured pattern, PIN, or password.

After five incorrect pattern/PIN/password attempts, wait 30 seconds before retrying. Contact the system administrator if the credential is forgotten.

Drag down the status bar to open notifications; drag down again to open Quick Settings. Use **Edit** to reorder tiles or drag additional controls into the panel. Tap the Settings icon to open device settings. Swipe up from the screen bottom or tap Home to close the notification panel.

## Home-Screen Applications, Folders, Widgets, and Wallpaper

Drag the application icon to the **Remove** area and release. The guide warns that removal also removes related user data and configuration files.

2. Drag it onto another icon and release.

1. Long-press an empty part of the Home screen and tap **Widgets**.

## USB File and Picture Transfer

For file transfer with a computer:

1. Connect U2S by USB.
2. Open the notification panel, tap the USB notification, and select **File Transfer**.
3. On the computer, open the U2S from **My Computer**.

# Chapter 7 - Maintenance and Troubleshooting

## Maintenance

- Do not scratch the glass touch screen or subject the device to drops or strong impact.
- Protect it from extreme temperatures and heat sources.
- Do not use or store it in dusty, damp, or wet locations.
- Clean the screen with a soft lens cloth; for soil, use a cloth moistened with diluted window-cleaning solution.
- For long-term storage, keep the battery at least 50% charged and store at **-40°C to 70°C**.

### General Cleaning

Do not apply liquid directly. Lightly dampen a soft cloth or use pre-moistened wipes. Do not wrap the device in the cloth. Prevent pooling around the display and other openings and air-dry before use.

Required materials: alcohol wipes, lens tissue, cotton-tipped applicators, isopropyl alcohol, and canned compressed air with a tube.

Cleaning frequency depends on the environment. In dirty environments, clean the camera window regularly.

- **Housing:** Wipe the housing and buttons with alcohol wipes.
- **Display:** Wipe with alcohol wipes without allowing liquid to pool at the edges; dry immediately with a soft, non-abrasive cloth.
- **Camera and exit window:** Periodically wipe with lens tissue or another optical-cleaning material.

### Device Connector Cleaning

1. Dip the cotton part of an applicator in isopropyl alcohol.
2. Rub it back and forth across the connector without leaving cotton residue.
3. Repeat at least three times.
4. Use another alcohol-dipped applicator to remove grease and dirt near the connector.
5. Repeat with a dry applicator.
6. Blow compressed air from about 1/2 inch away.
7. Inspect and repeat if grease or dirt remains.

### Cradle Connector Cleaning

1. Disconnect the cradle's DC power cable.
2. Dip an applicator in isopropyl alcohol.
3. Rub it slowly along the connector pins from side to side without leaving residue.
4. Clean all connector sides.
5. Blow compressed air from about 1/2 inch away.
6. Remove remaining lint.
7. Clean other cradle grease/dirt with a lint-free cloth and alcohol.
8. Let alcohol air-dry for 10 to 30 minutes before restoring power; low temperature and high humidity require longer, while warm, dry conditions require less.

## Factory Reset

A factory reset erases information, content, and settings from internal shared storage and IPSM card storage and returns the device to factory settings. Back up data first.

- For a device with eSIM, the reset may keep or erase it. Erasure requires carrier reactivation. eSIM availability depends on model, country, and region.
- If a Google Account is present, retain the Google username and password or the device cannot be used after reset. After recently changing the Google password, wait 24 hours before factory reset.
